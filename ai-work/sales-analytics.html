What is sales analytics, and what does it change for a bank or insurer?

Sales analytics turns sales data into decisions about where to spend effort11. At a bank, credit union or insurer, AI ranks who to call, what to offer and why1. Clean customer data and a holdout test make or break it12,6.

Who on your sales team keeps the decision?

  • Rep, relationship manager or licensed agent: picks who to call from the top of the list, and can skip anyone2,13. Their logged outcomes train the next version4.
  • Compliance officer: approves the offer list, targeting fields and exclusions before launch, and keeps the sign-off on file. Examiners review the criteria used to pick who gets prescreened offers8.
  • Head of retail banking or sales: sets goals on conversations and fit, not product counts14. Pays no incentive tied to products per customer without risk controls10.
  • Analyst or marketing lead: owns the list, the uncalled holdout group and the monthly lift report6,15.

What do your reps do once the model ranks the list?

  • Same people, better calls: commercial banking relationship managers with an analytics workbench grew portfolios 9%, against 5% for a control group1.
  • Less desk time: those managers spent 90% less time on account planning and got 5 times more cross-sell ideas1.
  • Most firms use AI to help staff: of firms reporting AI task effects, 66% only augment and 2% saw AI-related employment fall16,17.
  • New workflows and staff training are the most common changes firms make with AI, at about 15% each16.

Which way to build sales analytics fits your firm?

1. Community bank or credit union: first call list on the CRM you own

You have a CRM or marketing customer file and one analyst. Start with two products, such as auto refinance and home equity, and read the result after 60 days4,3.

  1. Data leadExport 24 months of holdings, balances and transactions from the core
  2. Data leadLink accounts into households
  3. AnalystBuild the white-space grid and a propensity score for two products
  4. Compliance officerReview targeting fields and the offer list, then sign off
  5. Head of retailSet goals on conversations and outcomes, not product counts
  6. RepsCall the top of the list, with the reason shown, and log each outcome
  7. AnalystCompare against the 10% uncalled group after 60 days

Cost: Use a CRM you own, or a listed one: HubSpot Sales Hub Pro is $90 a seat a month38. Dynamics 365 Sales Premium is $150 a user a month29.

2. Bank or credit union on a digital banking platform: bought module

You run Alkami or a similar platform and want campaigns plus scoring without a modelling team39. Vendor models still need validation and outcomes checks44.

  1. Head of retailPick the module tier
  2. Vendor and data leadConnect core and digital banking data
  3. Compliance officerAsk the vendor for its fair lending tests, the reason shown for each pick and its validation papers
  4. Marketing lead and repsRun campaigns and calls from the list, keeping a holdout
  5. AnalystEach quarter, check the lift and whether the scores still hold up

Cost: Alkami lists $23,000, $47,000 or $77,000 a year by tier, read 26 Nov 202639. Data connection work is extra.

3. Insurance agency: account rounding

An agency that wants each client's missing coverages ranked for producers26,2. Check your state's AI bulletin first45.

  1. Agency operationsConnect the agency management system to a rounding tool
  2. ProducerWork the ranked list of missing coverages
  3. Licensed agentJudge suitability before any offer
  4. Agency principalCheck state AI bulletin duties, such as Colorado's
  5. Agency operationsTrack the rounding rate against an uncalled holdout

Cost: AgencyKPI lists $250 a month, or $166.67 a month billed yearly, read 26 Nov 202626. OpSpring prices by credits and publishes no amounts2.

How do you test a call list before you trust it?

  1. Pilot one product in one branch or region. Record today's numbers there first: contacts made, offers taken, balances opened and rep time per conversation.
  2. Back-test: score last quarter's customers using data as of its first day. Check whether the top tenth really opened more products than the rest.
  3. Keep a random 10% of the top of the list uncalled. Lift is the called group's take-up minus the uncalled group's6.
  4. Also call a small random group below the cut-off. That tests the ranking itself, not just the calls15.
  5. If many top-scored customers buy without a call, switch to uplift targeting: aim at people a call actually persuades59,5.
  6. For credit products, compare who gets picked and what they are offered across groups, and document the review8. Insurers in Colorado must test for unfair discrimination58.
  7. Price the lift: extra balances or premium times margin, minus rep time and tool cost, over 90 days.
  8. Rescore monthly or quarterly. Propensity models need a periodic refresh60.

Where does sales analytics go wrong?

  • Quotas built on model lists invite abuse: Wells Fargo staff opened about 1.5 million unauthorized deposit accounts9. Reward conversations and fit, not product counts10,14.
  • Lookalike seeds copy the seed list's makeup. Meta bars lookalikes for financial product ads, and HUD flags them for housing35,36.
  • Targeting fields can stand in for a protected trait. Disparate treatment is still illegal, so compliance reviews each field before launch7,8.
  • Poor householding hides customers, and many banks haven't linked accounts into households12. Fix the data before you score it.
  • Response models chase people who would buy anyway, and some customers react badly to any contact5,15. Test with an uncalled group and consider uplift59.
  • A list with no reasons leaves reps unable to explain and compliance unable to review. Only 4 of the 9 vendor pages we checked showed a reason per pick13,2.

Questions buyers ask about Sales analytics

How do banks calculate share of wallet?

Divide what a customer keeps or borrows with you by their total across all institutions21,20. US consumers hold about 8.5 banking products across institutions, so most wallets are split61,62.

What is the difference between a propensity model and an uplift model?

A propensity model finds who is likely to buy; an uplift model finds who buys because you called59. Response models tend to target people who would have bought anyway5.

Can banks use lookalike audiences on Facebook?

Not for financial product ads. Meta's special ad category for financial products and services bars lookalike audiences in the US35,63.

Does the 2026 Regulation B rule mean targeting is unrestricted?

No. From 21 Jul 2026 it drops disparate-impact liability and limits discouragement to words and images7,50. Disparate treatment, the Fair Housing Act and state laws still apply7,36,66,67.

What does sales analytics software cost?

CRM seats with AI scoring list at $90 to $150 a user a month38,29. Alkami's bank marketing module starts at $23,000 a year39. Revenue intelligence tools such as Gong don't publish prices40,41.

Does sales analytics take over from our reps?

No: it ranks the list, and reps still choose who to call and what to say2,1. Of firms reporting AI task effects, 66% use it only to help staff16.

Should you buy sales analytics or build it?

Buy: Buy the scoring and workflow inside the CRM or digital banking platform you already run29,39. In one survey of enterprise buyers, 76% of AI use cases were bought rather than built64.

Build: Build the parts that are yours: the household data join, the holdout test and the fair lending review12,8. Few vendor pages we checked show these28. In MIT's study, outside partners reached deployment about twice as often as internal builds65.

When should you pick something else?

  • Few products or a small customer base: a spreadsheet white-space grid is enough to start24,25.
  • Already on Salesforce Financial Services Cloud, Dynamics 365 Sales Premium or HubSpot Sales Hub Pro: turn on the built-in scoring first29,38.
  • Insurance agency: try a rounding tool such as AgencyKPI before a custom model26.
  • Need to forecast the pipeline, not pick accounts: revenue intelligence tools such as Gong or Clari fit better40,41.
