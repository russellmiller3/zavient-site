What is sales analytics, and what does it change for a bank or insurer?

Sales analytics turns sales data into decisions; at a bank, credit union or insurer, AI ranks who to call, what to offer and why.11,1 Reps still choose who to call.2,13 Clean customer data and a holdout test make or break it.12,6

Who on your sales team keeps the decision?

The rep or licensed agent keeps the decision and can skip anyone, while compliance signs off targeting and the sales head sets goals on conversations and fit.2,8,14

  • The rep, relationship manager or licensed agent keeps it: they pick who to call from the top of the list, and can skip anyone.2,13 Their logged outcomes train the next version.4
  • Compliance officer: approves the offer list, targeting fields and exclusions before launch, and keeps the sign-off on file. Examiners review the criteria used to pick who gets prescreened offers.8
  • Head of retail banking or sales: sets goals on conversations and fit, not product counts.14 Pays no incentive tied to products per customer without risk controls.10
  • Analyst or marketing lead: owns the list, the uncalled holdout group and the monthly lift report.6,15

What do your reps do once the model ranks the list?

Reps still pick who to call and what to offer, but spend less time on account planning and get more cross-sell ideas to bring to each conversation.2,1

  • Reps call from the top of the list and spend less time on desk research, so more of their day goes to conversations.1
  • Same people, better calls: commercial banking relationship managers with an analytics workbench grew portfolios 9%, against 5% for a control group.1
  • Less desk time: those managers spent 90% less time on account planning and got 5 times more cross-sell ideas.1
  • Most firms use AI to help staff: of firms reporting AI task effects, 66% only augment and 2% saw AI-related employment fall.16,17
  • New workflows and staff training are the most common changes firms make with AI, at about 15% each.16

What are the six sales analytics methods, in plain words?

The six are share of wallet, white space analysis, propensity models, next best action, churn prediction and lookalike models, each answering one question about a customer.11,1

Which way to build sales analytics fits your firm?

Community banks and credit unions start with a call list on their own CRM, digital banking platform users buy a module, and insurance agencies list each client's missing coverages.4,39,2

1. Community bank or credit union: first call list on the CRM you own

You have a CRM or marketing customer file and one analyst. Start with two products, such as auto refinance and home equity, and read the result after 60 days.4,3

  1. Data leadExport 24 months of holdings, balances and transactions from the core
  2. Data leadLink accounts into households
  3. AnalystBuild the white-space grid and a propensity score for two products
  4. Compliance officerReview targeting fields and the offer list, then sign off
  5. Head of retailSet goals on conversations and outcomes, not product counts
  6. RepsCall the top of the list, with the reason shown, and log each outcome
  7. AnalystCompare against the 10% uncalled group after 60 days

Cost: Use a CRM you own, or a listed one: HubSpot Sales Hub Pro is $90 a seat a month.38 Dynamics 365 Sales Premium is $150 a user a month.29

2. Bank or credit union on a digital banking platform: bought module

You run Alkami or a similar platform and want campaigns plus scoring without a modelling team.39 Vendor models still need validation and outcomes checks.44

  1. Head of retailPick the module tier
  2. Vendor and data leadConnect core and digital banking data
  3. Compliance officerAsk the vendor for its fair lending tests, the reason shown for each pick and its validation papers
  4. Marketing lead and repsRun campaigns and calls from the list, keeping a holdout
  5. AnalystEach quarter, check the lift and whether the scores still hold up

Cost: Alkami shows no price on its page, so ask for an all-in year-one quote.39 Data connection work is extra.

3. Insurance agency: account rounding

An agency that wants each client's missing coverages ranked for producers.2 Check your state's AI bulletin first.45

  1. Agency operationsConnect the agency management system to a rounding tool
  2. ProducerWork the ranked list of missing coverages
  3. Licensed agentJudge suitability before any offer
  4. Agency principalCheck state AI bulletin duties, such as Colorado's
  5. Agency operationsTrack the rounding rate against an uncalled holdout

Cost: OpSpring publishes no price on its page.2 AgencyKPI, a rounding tool that did list one, has shut down.26

How do you test a call list before you trust it?

Pilot one product in one branch, back-test the scores on last quarter, keep a random group uncalled to measure lift, and price the gain over 90 days.6

  1. Pilot it on one product in one branch, keep part of the list uncalled, and compare take-up before you roll it out.6 Record today's numbers there first: contacts made, offers taken, balances opened and rep time per conversation.
  2. Back-test: score last quarter's customers using data as of its first day. Check whether the top tenth really opened more products than the rest.
  3. Keep a random 10% of the top of the list uncalled. Lift is the called group's take-up minus the uncalled group's.6
  4. Also call a small random group below the cut-off. That tests the ranking itself, not just the calls.15
  5. If many top-scored customers buy without a call, switch to uplift targeting: aim at people a call actually persuades.59,5
  6. For credit products, compare who gets picked and what they are offered across groups, and document the review.8 Insurers in Colorado must test for unfair discrimination.58
  7. Price the lift: extra balances or premium times margin, minus rep time and tool cost, over 90 days.
  8. Rescore monthly or quarterly. Propensity models need a periodic refresh.60

Where does sales analytics go wrong?

Sales analytics fails when quotas on model lists invite abuse, targeting fields stand in for protected traits, household data is messy, or lists chase people who'd buy anyway.9,7,12,5

  • It goes wrong through quota abuse, lookalike and proxy bias, bad household data, chasing people who would buy anyway, and lists with no reasons.9,8 Each fix is below.
  • Quotas built on model lists invite abuse: Wells Fargo staff opened about 1.5 million unauthorized deposit accounts.9 Reward conversations and fit, not product counts.10,14
  • Lookalike seeds copy the seed list's makeup. Meta bars lookalikes for financial product ads, and HUD flags them for housing.35,36
  • Targeting fields can stand in for a protected trait. Disparate treatment is still illegal, so compliance reviews each field before launch.7,8
  • Poor householding hides customers, and many banks haven't linked accounts into households.12 Fix the data before you score it.
  • Response models chase people who would buy anyway, and some customers react badly to any contact.5,15 Test with an uncalled group and consider uplift.59
  • A list with no reasons leaves reps unable to explain and compliance unable to review. Only 4 of the 9 vendor pages we checked showed a reason per pick.13,2

Questions buyers ask about Sales analytics

How do banks calculate share of wallet?

Divide what a customer keeps or borrows with you by their total across all institutions.21,20 US consumers hold about 8.5 banking products across institutions, so most wallets are split.61,62

What is the difference between a propensity model and an uplift model?

A propensity model finds who is likely to buy, while an uplift model finds who buys because you called.59 Response models tend to target people who would have bought anyway.5

Can banks use lookalike audiences on Facebook?

Not for financial product ads, because Meta's special ad category for financial products and services bars lookalike audiences in the US.35,63

Does the 2026 Regulation B rule mean targeting is unrestricted?

No: from 21 Jul 2026 it drops disparate-impact liability and limits discouragement to words and images.7,50 Disparate treatment, the Fair Housing Act and state laws still apply.7,36,66,67

What does sales analytics software cost?

CRM seats with AI scoring list at $90 to $150 a user a month.38,29 Bank marketing modules such as Alkami's show no price on their pages.39 Revenue intelligence tools such as Gong don't publish prices.40,41

Does sales analytics take over from our reps?

No: it ranks the list, and reps still choose who to call and what to say.2,1 Of firms reporting AI task effects, 66% use it only to help staff.16

Should you buy sales analytics or build it?

Buy: Mostly buy the scoring and workflow inside the CRM or digital banking platform you already run.29,39 In one survey of enterprise buyers, 76% of AI use cases were bought rather than built.64

Build: Build the parts that are yours: the household data join, the holdout test and the fair lending review.12,8 Few vendor pages we checked show these.28 In MIT's study, outside partners reached deployment about twice as often as internal builds.65

When should you pick something else?

Pick something else when a spreadsheet grid is enough, your CRM already has built-in scoring, an agency can start with a missing-coverage list, or you need pipeline forecasting instead.24,29,2,40

  • When you have few products or a small customer base: a spreadsheet white-space grid is enough to start.24,25
  • Already on Salesforce Financial Services Cloud, Dynamics 365 Sales Premium or HubSpot Sales Hub Pro: turn on the built-in scoring first.29,38
  • Insurance agency: try a tool that lists each client's missing coverages before a custom model.2
  • Need to forecast the pipeline, not pick accounts: revenue intelligence tools such as Gong or Clari fit better.40,41
