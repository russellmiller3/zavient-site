AI underwriting: can it help underwriters, and who is using it?

Bottom line
  • The money is hours back for deciding. P&C underwriters spend about 39% of their time on admin and other non-core work, the paperwork AI can take on1,8,7.
  • Start with the reading: statements and tax returns for lenders, broker submissions for commercial insurers and MGAs (managing general agents)22, referred-case evidence for life insurers19.
  • AI reads long financial PDFs well: the two best models made zero mistakes on 30 fund reports, answers locked in advance13. Those weren't loan files, so benchmark on your own13.
  • Keep the decision with an explainable model and a named underwriter. US lenders owe decline reasons; New York expects the same from insurers4,17,2.
  • First step: benchmark AI on 50 past files from one line or one branch before you buy. Vendors' faster-quote figures are their own customers' claims21,28.

What does AI underwriting do beyond scanning documents?

AI underwriting does the reading: it pulls the facts from applications, statements and broker emails so your underwriters decide faster13,22. It fits small-business lenders, life insurers with a rules engine20, and commercial insurers and MGAs whose underwriters spend hours keying broker submissions7. P&C underwriters spend about 4 in 10 hours on admin, and the decision stays explainable with a person accountable1,7,4,2.

Where does AI fit in your underwriting workflow?

1. Commercial insurance: submission intake

Commercial insurers and MGAs whose underwriters spend hours keying broker submissions; admin takes 41% to 43% of their time7.

  1. Broker email, forms and loss runs (claims history) land in a queue the ops lead owns
  2. AI extracts each fact with page and quote
  3. AI checks appetite (the risks you want to write) and clearance (has another broker already sent this risk?); an underwriting assistant resolves flagged facts
  4. AI adds third-party data
  5. Underwriter works the queue best fit first and decides

Cost: Reading cost $0.12 to $0.15 per fund report with the two best models in our benchmark, not per loan file13. Intake products publish no price24; a Zavient build is quoted per job, not listed here.

2. Small-business lending: file to decision

Lenders whose underwriters spend the day reading statements and returns.

  1. Bank statements and tax returns land in a queue the ops lead owns
  2. AI spreads statements and returns into your template, meaning it copies each figure into your standard credit-analysis layout
  3. An underwriting assistant clears flagged facts
  4. Explainable model drafts a score with its reasons
  5. Underwriter decides; reasons logged

Cost: Under $0.01 to about $0.30 per fund report to read with current models in our benchmark, not per loan file13. The build, including a scorecard you own, is quoted per job, not listed here.

3. Life insurance: evidence summary for the rules engine

Life and health carriers with an existing rules engine.

  1. Application and medical evidence land in a queue the ops lead owns
  2. AI summarises with page citations
  3. Rules engine applies your manual
  4. Clean casesOffer drafted, an underwriter spot-checks a sample
  5. OthersReferred to an underwriter, who decides

Cost: Rules engines such as MagnumXP and ALLFINANZ publish no price; you contact the vendor18,20. The AI reading layer is quoted per job, and you can benchmark and swap it.

How do you benchmark an AI underwriting tool before you buy?

  1. Before any AI, time 50 files from one line or one branch as your team handles them today. Record hours per file, decision turnaround and how many in-appetite files are left without a quote or decision.
  2. Then take 50 past files with known outcomes, including your messiest scans and longest packets.
  3. Have your underwriters write the right value for every fact before any AI reads the files, and lock that answer key.
  4. Run each tool on the same files. Count wrong facts apart from blanks, and require a page and quote for every fact.
  5. For scoring, compare an explainable model with your current method on the same past files. Check that the decline reasons make sense to an underwriter.
  6. Measure cost and time per file, and how many files still need a person. Report files decided per underwriter per week, decision turnaround and in-appetite submissions left unquoted, before and after.

Where does AI underwriting go wrong?

  • You stay responsible for a vendor's model. New York and the NAIC bulletin both expect audit rights in vendor contracts where available2,34.
  • Models can carry past bias into new decisions. New York and Colorado expect insurers to test for unfair outcomes across groups before use and on a schedule after2,16.
  • A model's confidence score is not a warning sign. In our benchmark, wrong answers came back rated 70 to 10013.
  • Vendor claims such as faster quotes are the vendor's own figures24, often from chosen customers21,28. Ask for a benchmark on your own files.
  • Flagged facts need an owner: in our benchmark, look-alike tables tripped 7 of 10 tools on one report13. Count flags per file, multiply by weekly files and name who clears them.

Should you buy an AI underwriting tool or build one?

Buy: Buy when your line is standard, the vendor already handles your submission formats, and you are happy to run inside its workbench.

Build: Build when your own loss history and appetite are your edge, or when you need the reading step to feed systems you already own. The reading layer is swappable: in our benchmark the top nine models were within 2 facts of each other13. The decision logic is yours.

When should you start somewhere else?

  • Consumer lending where the score is the product: start with the AI credit scoring guide30. The decline reasons are the regulated part4,17.
  • Life and health insurer: start from a rules engine such as Swiss Re MagnumXP or Munich Re ALLFINANZ18,20. AI earns its keep summarising the evidence on referred cases so the underwriter decides faster, not making the call19,20.
  • Commercial insurer or MGA swamped by broker submissions: an off-the-shelf intake product (Cytora, Convr, FurtherAI, Sixfold) may beat a build23,24,22,21. Benchmark one on 50 of your own past submissions before you sign.
  • The question is price, not whether to take the risk: look at actuarial pricing platforms such as Akur8, Earnix or hyperexponential25,26,27.
