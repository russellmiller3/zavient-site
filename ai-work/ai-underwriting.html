AI underwriting: can it help underwriters, and who is using it?

Bottom line
  • Property and casualty (P&C) underwriters spend about 4 in 10 hours on admin AI can take on1,7.
  • Start AI on the reading: statements, tax returns, broker submissions and referred-case evidence21,18.

  • A named underwriter keeps the decision, with reasons a regulator can check4,2.

What does AI underwriting do beyond scanning documents?

AI underwriting does the reading: it pulls facts from applications, statements and broker emails12,21. Underwriters decide faster, and a named person still decides4,2. It fits small-business lenders, life insurers with a rules engine19, and commercial insurers swamped by broker submissions7.

Where does your team stay in charge?

  • Underwriter: decides whether to offer, decline or price, after reviewing what the software recommends.34 In New York, an adverse decision notice should name the information used and its source.37,2
  • Credit officer at a lender: owns each decline and its reasons.4 For a scoring model, those reasons must be the factors it actually scored.16
  • Head of underwriting: sits on the AI committee with claims, compliance and legal.38,31 Checking AI against expert review is one validation method the bulletin names.38
  • Model reviewer at a bank lender: an expert independent of the builder challenges a scoring model before first use.40 Bank regulators' 2026 guidance calls this "effective challenge" and leaves generative AI out of scope.40,39

What does your team do after?

  • Referred and complex files become the core job.18 BLS already lists reviewing software recommendations among underwriters' duties.34
  • Underwriters get back admin hours: 39% to 43% of their time goes to admin today.1,7
  • Flagged gaps go back to people: underwriters contact field representatives, medical staff and others for what the file lacks.34,12
  • Testing for unfair outcomes, before use and on a schedule after, becomes standing work.2,15 Among US firms using AI, 66% use it only to help staff; 2% saw AI-related employment fall.36

Where does AI fit in your underwriting workflow?

1. Commercial insurance: submission intake

Commercial insurers and MGAs (managing general agents, which underwrite for a carrier) whose underwriters key in broker submissions. Admin takes 41% to 43% of their time.7

  1. Broker email, forms and loss runs (claims history) land in a queue the ops lead owns
  2. AI extracts each fact with page and quote
  3. AI checks appetite (the risks you want to write) and clearance (has another broker already sent this risk?); an underwriting assistant resolves flagged facts
  4. AI adds third-party data
  5. Underwriter works the queue best fit first and decides

Cost: Reading: about 12 to 15 cents a long fund report in our test12. Intake products publish no price23; a Zavient build is quoted per job, not listed here.

2. Small-business lending: file to decision

Lenders whose underwriters spend the day reading statements and returns.

  1. Bank statements and tax returns land in a queue the ops lead owns
  2. AI spreads statements and returns into your template, meaning it copies each figure into your standard credit-analysis layout
  3. An underwriting assistant clears flagged facts
  4. Explainable model drafts a score with its reasons
  5. Underwriter decides; reasons logged

Cost: Reading: under 1 cent to about 30 cents a long fund report in our test12. The build, including a scorecard you own, is quoted per job, not listed here.

3. Life insurance: evidence summary for the rules engine

Life and health carriers with an existing rules engine.

  1. Application and medical evidence land in a queue the ops lead owns
  2. AI summarises with page citations
  3. Rules engine applies your manual
  4. Clean casesOffer drafted, an underwriter spot-checks a sample
  5. OthersReferred to an underwriter, who decides

Cost: Rules engines such as MagnumXP and ALLFINANZ publish no price; you contact the vendor17,19. The AI reading layer is quoted per job, and you can benchmark and swap it.

How do you benchmark an AI underwriting tool before you buy?

  1. Before any AI, time 50 files from one line or one branch as your team handles them today. Record hours per file, decision turnaround and how many in-appetite files are left without a quote or decision.
  2. Then take 50 past files with known outcomes, including your messiest scans and longest packets.
  3. Have your underwriters write the right value for every fact before any AI reads the files, and lock that answer key.
  4. Run each tool on the same files. Count wrong facts apart from blanks, and require a page and quote for every fact.
  5. For scoring, compare an explainable model with your current method on the same past files. Check that the decline reasons make sense to an underwriter.
  6. Measure cost and time per file, and how many files still need a person. Report files decided per underwriter per week, decision turnaround and in-appetite submissions left unquoted, before and after.
  7. Multiply hours saved per file by files per week and your loaded hourly cost. That gives the weekly dollar value of the change.

Where does AI underwriting go wrong?

  • You stay responsible for a vendor's model. New York and the NAIC bulletin both expect audit rights in vendor contracts where available2,31.
  • Models can carry past bias into new decisions. New York and Colorado expect insurers to test for unfair outcomes across groups before use and on a schedule after2,15.
  • A model's confidence score is not a warning sign. In our benchmark, wrong answers came back rated 70 to 10012.
  • Vendor claims such as faster quotes are the vendor's own figures23, often from chosen customers20,27. Ask for a benchmark on your own files.
  • Flagged facts need an owner: in our benchmark, look-alike tables tripped 7 of 10 tools on one report12. Count flags per file, multiply by weekly files and name who clears them.

Questions buyers ask about AI underwriting

What is AI underwriting?

AI reads the file and pulls out each fact with its page and quote.12,21 It can draft a risk score with its reasons for the underwriter to review.34 The underwriter still decides.34,2

Can AI approve or decline an application on its own?

For simple consumer loans, underwriting may be fully automated, BLS says.35 Either way, the lender still owes specific reasons for a decline.4 Insurers must hold vendor AI to the legal standards they meet themselves.38

Will AI take underwriters' jobs?

BLS projects insurance underwriter jobs to fall 4% from 2025 to 2035, citing automated underwriting software.34 It still expects about 6,800 openings a year.34 Among US firms using AI, 66% use it only to help staff; 2% saw AI-related employment fall.36

Do you have to explain a loan decline made with AI?

Yes. Reg B requires specific reasons, and for a scoring model they must be the factors it scored.4,16 The CFPB withdrew its 2022 circular on AI declines in May 2025, but the rule itself still applies.41,4

How accurate is AI at reading underwriting files?

On clean documents, very: the best model in a Fraunhofer study got 96.5% of invoice fields right.42 Scans are harder: 87.5% on scanned receipts.42 In our own test, two models made zero mistakes on 30 long fund reports.12

What does AI underwriting software cost?

None of the 19 products we checked publishes a price.23,29 Reading a long report cost under 1 cent to about 30 cents in our test.12 Budget from a quote scoped to one line, after a test on 50 of your files.20

Should you buy an AI underwriting tool or build one?

Buy: Buy when your line is standard, the vendor already handles your submission formats, and you are happy to run inside its workbench.22 Most firms buy: 76% of enterprise AI uses are bought, not built.43

Build: Build when your own loss history and appetite are your edge, or when you need the reading step to feed systems you already own. The reading layer is swappable: in our benchmark the top nine models were within 2 facts of each other12. The decision logic is yours.

When should you start somewhere else?

  • Consumer lending where the score is the product: start with the AI credit scoring guide29. The decline reasons are the regulated part4,16.
  • Life and health insurer: start from a rules engine such as Swiss Re MagnumXP or Munich Re ALLFINANZ17,19. AI earns its keep summarising the evidence on referred cases so the underwriter decides faster, not making the call18,19.
  • Swamped by broker submissions? An off-the-shelf intake tool may beat a build, if it passes a test on 50 past submissions22,21.
  • The question is price, not whether to take the risk: look at actuarial pricing platforms such as Akur8, Earnix or hyperexponential24,25,26.
