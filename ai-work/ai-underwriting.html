What does AI underwriting do beyond scanning documents?

AI underwriting reads the file and drafts the work: facts from applications, spreads of tax returns, and a first-draft credit memo or risk score10,19,45. A named underwriter or credit officer still decides3,1. It fits community and commercial lenders, life insurers with a rules engine17, and commercial insurers swamped by submissions6.

Where does your team stay in charge?

Your team keeps every underwriting decision: the underwriter offers, declines or prices, the credit analyst owns the numbers and rating, and the credit officer owns each decline's reasons32,51,3. An independent reviewer challenges the model first, and the head of underwriting sits on the AI committee38,36.

  • Underwriter: decides whether to offer, decline or price, after reviewing what the software recommends.32 In New York, an adverse decision notice should name the information used and its source.35,1
  • Credit officer at a lender: owns each decline and its reasons.3 For a scoring model, those reasons must be the factors it actually scored.14
  • Credit analyst at a commercial lender: ties out flagged figures and decides global cash flow add-backs and which entities count.51,50 The analyst writes the risk discussion, recommendation and proposed risk rating; the credit officer or committee approves.46,50
  • Head of underwriting: sits on the AI committee with claims, compliance and legal.36,29 Checking AI against expert review is one validation method the bulletin names.36
  • Model reviewer at a bank lender: an expert independent of the builder challenges a scoring model before first use.38 Bank regulators' 2026 guidance calls this "effective challenge" and leaves generative AI out of scope.38,37

What does your team do after?

After AI does the reading, your team works the referred and complex files, chases what the file lacks, and tests for unfair outcomes on a schedule16,32,1,13. Underwriters also get back admin time, now 41% to 43% of their week6.

  • Referred and complex files become the core job.16 BLS (the Bureau of Labor Statistics) already lists reviewing software recommendations among underwriters' duties.32
  • Underwriters get back admin hours: 41% to 43% of their time goes to admin today, per Capgemini.6 Credit analysts move from typing spreads to judging global cash flow and the risk rating.51,46
  • Flagged gaps go back to people: underwriters contact field representatives, medical staff and others for what the file lacks.32,10
  • Testing for unfair outcomes, before use and on a schedule after, becomes standing work.1,13 Among US firms using AI, 66% use it only to help staff; 2% saw AI-related employment fall.34

Can AI pre-underwrite a loan file?

Yes, up to the decision: AI can pull revenue, debts and risks from statements and tax returns, each with its page and quote, and draft a score with reasons10,12. The underwriter accepts or changes it and makes the call3,14.

A small-business loan application, 12 months of bank statements and two years of tax returns.Average monthly revenue, existing debt payments and two flagged risks, each with its page and quote. Plus a draft risk score with its main reasons, for the underwriter to accept or change.

Can AI draft a commercial credit memo, and what does the analyst still own?

Yes, AI can sort the borrower package, spread the figures and draft a cited first memo45,47. The analyst still owns the tie-out, global cash flow calls, risk discussion and rating, and the credit officer or committee decides51,50.

At TD, a large bank, an AI tool drafts six cited memo sections in under 30 minutes, against up to three working days by hand45. That is the bank's own figure, so treat it as a ceiling. We found no independent time study at community-bank scale; vendor figures are labelled47,46.

Where does AI fit in your underwriting workflow?

AI fits the reading and drafting steps: commercial submission intake, spreading and credit memo drafts, and life-insurance evidence summaries6,10,45,16. The underwriter or credit officer still decides in each one3,1.

1. Commercial insurance: submission intake

Commercial insurers and MGAs (managing general agents, which underwrite for a carrier) whose underwriters key in broker submissions. Admin takes 41% to 43% of their time.6

  1. Broker email, forms and loss runs (claims history) land in a queue the ops lead owns
  2. AI extracts each fact with page and quote
  3. AI checks appetite (the risks you want to write) and clearance (has another broker already sent this risk?); an underwriting assistant resolves flagged facts
  4. AI adds third-party data
  5. Underwriter works the queue best fit first and decides

Cost: Reading: about 12 to 15 cents a long fund report in our test10. Intake products publish no price21; a Zavient build is quoted per job, not listed here.

2. Small-business lending: file to decision

Lenders whose underwriters spend the day reading statements and returns.

  1. Bank statements and tax returns land in a queue the ops lead owns
  2. AI spreads statements and returns into your template, meaning it copies each figure into your standard credit-analysis layout
  3. An underwriting assistant clears flagged facts
  4. Explainable model drafts a score with its reasons
  5. Underwriter decides; reasons logged

Cost: Reading: under 1 cent to about 30 cents a long fund report in our test10. The build, including a scorecard you own, is quoted per job, not listed here.

3. Community or commercial bank: credit memo from the borrower package

Banks and credit unions whose analysts spend days spreading returns and writing memos. A memo takes 8+ hours to weeks, per Moody's (vendor).46

  1. The borrower package lands in a queue the credit administration lead owns
  2. AI spreads returns and statements, citing a page per figure; the analyst ties out flagged figures
  3. AI drafts global cash flow; the analyst decides add-backs and which entities count
  4. AI drafts the memo sections; the analyst writes the risk discussion, recommendation and rating
  5. Credit officer or loan committee decides; AI then tracks covenants and statement due dates

Cost: TD cut first drafts from up to three days to under 30 minutes, a large bank's own figure.45 We found no independent community-bank time study, so time 50 files from one lending team first.

4. Life insurance: evidence summary for the rules engine

Life and health carriers with an existing rules engine.

  1. Application and medical evidence land in a queue the ops lead owns
  2. AI summarises with page citations
  3. Rules engine applies your manual
  4. Clean casesOffer drafted, an underwriter spot-checks a sample
  5. OthersReferred to an underwriter, who decides

Cost: Rules engines such as MagnumXP and ALLFINANZ publish no price; you contact the vendor15,17. The AI reading layer is quoted per job, and you can benchmark and swap it.

What will examiners and regulators ask about AI underwriting?

Examiners will ask whether your analyst checked global cash flow, covenants and the file, and regulators want specific decline reasons, fairness tests and vendor oversight50,51,3,1,29. The 2026 bank model guidance leaves generative AI out of scope37,38. In the EU, regulators will also ask for logs and human oversight from 2 Dec 202730,31,8.

How do you benchmark an AI underwriting tool before you buy?

Lock an underwriter-written answer key on 50 past files, run each tool on the same files, and count wrong facts, cost and time per file10. Time the same files done by hand first, so you have a before number.

  1. Before any AI, time 50 files from one line or one branch as your team handles them today. Record hours per file, decision turnaround and how many in-appetite files are left without a quote or decision.
  2. Then take 50 past files with known outcomes, including your messiest scans and longest packets.
  3. Have your underwriters write the right value for every fact before any AI reads the files, and lock that answer key.
  4. Run each tool on the same files. Count wrong facts apart from blanks, and require a page and quote for every fact.
  5. For scoring, compare an explainable model with your current method on the same past files. Check that the decline reasons make sense to an underwriter.
  6. Measure cost and time per file, and how many files still need a person. Report files decided per underwriter per week, decision turnaround and in-appetite submissions left unquoted, before and after.
  7. Multiply hours saved per file by files per week and your loaded hourly cost. That gives the weekly dollar value of the change.

Where does AI underwriting go wrong?

AI underwriting goes wrong when models carry past bias, report high confidence on wrong answers, take the wrong look-alike table, or arrive with vendor speed claims you never tested1,13,10,21. You also stay responsible for any vendor's model1,29.

  • You stay responsible for a vendor's model. New York and the NAIC bulletin both expect audit rights in vendor contracts where available1,29.
  • Models can carry past bias into new decisions. New York and Colorado expect insurers to test for unfair outcomes across groups before use and on a schedule after1,13.
  • A model's confidence score is not a warning sign. In our benchmark, wrong answers came back rated 70 to 10010.
  • Credit memo drafts can carry wrong figures inside a confident, well-written narrative48,49. Tie out every figure that feeds debt service coverage before the memo goes to committee.
  • Vendor claims such as faster quotes are the vendor's own figures21, often from chosen customers18,25. Ask for a benchmark on your own files.
  • Flagged facts need an owner: in our benchmark, look-alike tables tripped 7 of 10 tools on one report10. Count flags per file, multiply by weekly files and name who clears them.

Questions buyers ask about AI underwriting

What is AI underwriting?

AI underwriting is software that reads the file and pulls out each fact with its page and quote.10,19 It can draft a risk score with its reasons for the underwriter to review.32 The underwriter still decides.32,1

Are banks using AI for underwriting?

Yes, but mostly in pilots: half of community banks using or planning AI name underwriting or risk modeling as a use.42 A vendor survey of community lenders found 21% using AI in underwriting or risk review.43 In a San Francisco Fed sample, 84% of banks under $10B had never posted an AI-related job.44

Can AI draft a commercial credit memo for a community bank?

Yes, a first draft: TD's tool drafts six cited sections in under 30 minutes, and 95% passed expert review.45 That is a large bank's figure; we found no independent community-bank study. The analyst still writes the recommendation and risk rating.46,50

How do you prepare a credit memo with AI?

Spread the financials, draft global cash flow and the cited sections, then have the analyst tie out figures and write the risk discussion and rating.47,51,46 After the decision, check the commitment letter against the approved terms.50

Which AI is best for credit analysis?

The best tool for your lender cites every figure to its page, fits your template and passes a test on 50 of your files.10 Language models can state wrong figures in a tidy narrative, and errors rise with calculation depth.48,49 We don't rank vendors; test them side by side.

Can AI help with loan review?

Yes, as a pre-reader: AI can summarise the file and draft the review worksheet and exception list, per one loan review vendor.54 The independent reviewer sets final ratings and findings under the 2020 interagency guidance.53 Portfolio-level scope and sampling are on our community banks page.

Can AI approve or decline an application on its own?

Only for simple consumer loans, where BLS says underwriting may be fully automated.33 Either way, the lender still owes specific reasons for a decline.3 Insurers must hold vendor AI to the legal standards they meet themselves.36

Will underwriting get replaced by AI?

No, not most of it: BLS projects insurance underwriter jobs to fall 4% from 2025 to 2035, with about 6,800 openings a year.32 Banks that use AI heavily lean on hard data and do less small-business lending, where a lender's local knowledge counts.44 Among US firms using AI, 66% use it only to help staff; 2% saw AI-related employment fall.34

Do you have to explain a loan decline made with AI?

Yes: Reg B requires specific reasons, and for a scoring model they must be the factors it scored.3,14 The CFPB withdrew its 2022 circular on AI declines in May 2025, but the rule itself still applies.39,3

How accurate is AI at reading underwriting files?

AI is very accurate at reading clean documents: the best model in a Fraunhofer study got 96.5% of invoice fields right.40 Scans are harder: 87.5% on scanned receipts.40 In our own test, two models made zero mistakes on 30 long fund reports.10

What does AI underwriting software cost?

AI underwriting is sold by quote, with no list price among 19 products we checked; reading a report cost under 1 cent to 30 cents.21,27,10 The reading cost is from our test on long fund reports.10 Budget from a quote scoped to one line of business, after a test on 50 of your files.18

Should you buy an AI underwriting tool or build one?

Buy when your line is standard and the vendor already reads your submission formats.20 Build when your own loss history is your edge, because the reading layer is swappable10.

Buy: Buy when your line is standard, the vendor already handles your submission formats, and you are happy to run inside its workbench.20 Most firms buy: 76% of enterprise AI uses are bought, not built.41

Build: Build when your own loss history and appetite are your edge, or when you need the reading step to feed systems you already own. The reading layer is swappable: in our benchmark the top nine models were within 2 facts of each other10. The decision logic is yours.

When should you start somewhere else?

Start somewhere else for consumer lending where the score is the product, life and health underwriting, or pricing questions27,15,22. Those point to the AI credit scoring guide, a rules engine such as Swiss Re MagnumXP, or a pricing platform such as Akur827,15,22.

  • Consumer lending where the score is the product: start with the AI credit scoring guide27. The decline reasons are the regulated part3,14.
  • Life and health insurer: start from a rules engine such as Swiss Re MagnumXP or Munich Re ALLFINANZ15,17. AI earns its keep summarising the evidence on referred cases so the underwriter decides faster, not making the call16,17.
  • Swamped by broker submissions? An off-the-shelf intake tool may beat a build, if it passes a test on 50 past submissions20,19.
  • The question is price, not whether to take the risk: look at actuarial pricing platforms such as Akur8, Earnix or hyperexponential22,23,24.
