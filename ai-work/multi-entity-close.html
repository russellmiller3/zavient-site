What is consolidated financial reporting, and what can AI change?

Consolidated financial reporting turns each company's books into one set of group statements, with sales and balances between them eliminated.19,18 AI suggests account mappings and intercompany matches, and the controller approves each one.6,12 The hard part sits upstream: different ledgers, charts of accounts and intercompany that won't tie.34

Where does your controller stay in charge?

The controller stays in charge: AI suggests account mappings and intercompany matches, and the controller approves each one and signs off eliminations and statements.6,5,8

  • Controller: approves the mapping from each add-on's accounts to the group chart, then every new account.12,13 AI only suggests; nothing posts without that approval.
  • Controller: approves each intercompany match the AI suggests, and every low-confidence or unmatched pair.6,5 Breaks go back to the add-on's bookkeeper with context.5
  • Controller plus a second reviewer: sign off eliminations and every top-level adjustment.18,26 Auditors evaluate journal entries and adjustments in the period-end process.20
  • Head of finance ops: signs off the consolidated and consolidating statements and owns the lender package.3,4 Auditors will assess how AI affects your controls.8

What does your finance team do once AI takes the first pass?

Your finance team moves from keying mappings and chasing matches to reviewing AI suggestions, fixing routed breaks and running the close checklist.7,5,16

  • The controller moves from keying mappings to reviewing suggestions and exceptions. Surveyed CFOs already spend an average 26% of the workday checking AI output (Datarails, vendor).7
  • Add-on bookkeepers keep their own ledgers and fix the breaks routed back to them.5
  • The close owner runs a workday checklist with named owners, built back from the lender's deadline.16,2
  • Headcount isn't the goal: of firms using AI, 66% only use it to help staff.9 Thin teams are the bigger risk; Cambrex's finance team was too thinly staffed to reconcile intercompany.23

Which way to consolidate fits your platform?

Few add-ons on QuickBooks or Xero fit a reporting tool.30 Mixed ledgers need a mapping layer with reviewed AI matching, and a large group fits an enterprise performance management (EPM) suite.31,5

1. Few add-ons on QuickBooks Online or Xero, little intercompany

Two to a few dozen companies on the same ledger family, with few sales between them. A reporting tool can pull and add up the books.30

  1. ControllerSet one group chart of accounts
  2. Add-on bookkeeperClose local books by workday 3 (the third business day after month end)
  3. ControllerApprove the suggested mapping in the tool
  4. Finance ops leadPull trial balances and run the consolidation
  5. ControllerReview and sign the consolidated statements
  6. Head of finance opsSend the lender package

Cost: Fathom lists $450 a month for 25 companies, plus $17 per extra, read 26 Nov 2026.30 Add the controller's setup time for the group chart.

2. Platform on mixed ledgers with real intercompany

Three or more add-ons on different ledgers, with shared services, management fees or stock moving between them. The lender wants consolidating statements within 30 days.3,2

  1. Finance ops leadPull trial balances from each ledger
  2. ControllerKey the mapping on entity plus local account
  3. AISuggest a group account for each local account, with a score
  4. ControllerApprove the mapping table, then lock and version it
  5. ControllerGive every company one counterparty code, the tag used on sales between group companies
  6. AISuggest intercompany matches on amount, date window, reference and currency
  7. ControllerApprove every match; route breaks to the add-on bookkeeper
  8. ControllerReview the drafted eliminations, with a second reviewer for top-level (topside) entries
  9. Head of finance opsSign the consolidating workbook and lender package

Cost: Software runs from Fathom's $450 a month30 to Oracle's EPM suite at $250 per named user a month, 10-user minimum.31 The mapping and intercompany setup is scoped per platform in the diagnostic.

3. Larger group moving to an EPM suite

Many entities, several currencies and an audit or sale on the horizon. Suites such as Oracle's ship their own intercompany agent (vendor).5

  1. ControllerClean and approve the mapping before migration
  2. ITLoad approved mappings and history into the suite
  3. AISettle routine disputes within policy and route the rest
  4. ControllerApprove matches and review the audit trail
  5. Head of finance opsSign the statements and lender package

Cost: Oracle's EPM suite lists $250 per named user a month, 10-user minimum, read 26 Nov 2026.31 Other suites quote per contract.

Which rules shape a consolidated close?

ASC 810 sets what a consolidation must show, while auditing standards and AICPA and COSO guidance set how auditors judge the close, AI steps included.19,20,8,22

ASC 810, ConsolidationIn forcePresent the group as one economic entity, mainly for owners and creditors.19 Balances and transactions between group companies are eliminated in full, even with a minority owner.18 Your mapping and intercompany rules exist to make that true every month.

How do you test AI on your close before you trust it?

Run one closed month through AI mapping and matching on two add-ons, compare it with the signed manual close, and trace every elimination to an approver.1,20

  1. Record today's numbers on the last closed month: workdays from first trial balance to consolidated statements, hours spent and late adjustments. APQC's median is 6 days.1
  2. Pick two add-ons on different ledgers, at least one with intercompany activity.
  3. Run AI mapping on both. Measure the share of accounts suggested above your confidence threshold, and the share the controller changed.12
  4. Run intercompany matching on the same month. Count the match rate, false matches caught in review and unmatched items by age.
  5. Compare with the manual close you already signed: same balances, same eliminations, fewer workdays? The target for false matches posted is zero.
  6. Run a control check: can an auditor trace each elimination to a source and an approver?20,8
  7. Price the lift: workdays freed inside the lender's 30-day clock,2 controller hours moved to review, and how much sooner each add-on's margins reach you.
  8. Decide with the controller to keep, change or stop.

Where does a multi-entity close go wrong?

A multi-entity close goes wrong when AI mappings post unreviewed, intercompany breaks age without owners, eliminations get missed, or top-level entries skip a second approver.12,23,24,26

  • Off-the-shelf models pick badly from thousands of options: under 19% right linking facts to the full US-GAAP taxonomy.13 Limit choices to your group chart and show the top candidates for approval.14
  • A company the model hasn't seen maps worse: in one study, transactions were classified 64.6% right, against 80.5% within known companies.12 Review every mapping for each new add-on.
  • Messy inputs raise AI errors: over 8% scaling errors from text, against 0.11% from structured data.27 Feed trial balances as data, not PDFs.
  • Missed eliminations overstate the group: INTL FCStone overstated sales by $1.3B and $2.6B in two years.24 Eliminate every month, not at year end.
  • Breaks pile up without hands: Cambrex logged intercompany imbalances, but its finance team was too thinly staffed to reconcile them.23 Give every break an owner and an age.
  • Top-level entries can skip review: split duties failed at CIRCOR, allowing $11M+ in unsupported entries.26 Have a second person approve each one.

Questions buyers ask about consolidated financial reporting

What are consolidated financial statements?

Consolidated statements show a parent and the companies it controls as one economic entity, mainly for owners and creditors.19 Balances and sales between those companies are eliminated in full.18

What's the difference between combined and consolidated financial statements?

Consolidated statements roll subsidiaries into a parent that controls them, while combined statements join sister companies under common control with no parent between them.19 The preparation steps are the same.19

Do we need to move every add-on to one ERP first?

No, map each add-on's accounts to one group chart now and decide later, company by company, whether to migrate.16,14

Can AI match intercompany transactions on its own?

Only routine items: Oracle says its agent settles routine disputes within policy, and BlackLine's suggests matches for a person to confirm (both vendor claims).5,6 Only 4% of CFOs in one vendor survey trust AI with month-end close without human review.7

How long should a multi-entity close take?

Six calendar days is the median from first trial balance to monthly consolidated statements.1 Most lender agreements we counted want statements within 30 days.2

What happens if intercompany doesn't eliminate?

Group sales and costs come out overstated, because sales between your own companies stay in.24 INTL FCStone's missed eliminations overstated sales by $2.6B in one year, corrected in a restatement.24 EIDP misclassified intercompany loan cash flows and reported a material weakness.25

Should you buy consolidation software or build the work around it?

Buy the consolidation software, and build the mapping, intercompany rules and review steps around it.28

Buy: Buy the consolidation software: most enterprise AI is bought, and bought tools succeed more often.28,29 Pick by entity count and ledger mix, from Fathom to an EPM suite.30,31

Build: Build the work no tool ships: the group chart, the mapping table, counterparty codes and the review log. Auditors will assess how far your statements rely on AI and how it affects controls.8

When should you pick something else?

Pick something else when your add-ons share one simple ledger, one ERP already holds every entity, a large group needs an EPM suite, or only reconciliations hurt.30,31

  • A few add-ons, all on QuickBooks Online or Xero, with little intercompany: a reporting tool such as Fathom may be enough. It lists $450 a month for 25 companies.30
  • One ERP already holds every entity: use its own multi-entity features, such as Sage Intacct or NetSuite OneWorld, before adding a layer. Most AI uses are bought, not built.28
  • A large group with many currencies and an EPM budget: Oracle's EPM suite lists $250 per named user a month, 10-user minimum.31 It ships its own intercompany agent (vendor).5
  • Only reconciliations hurt, not consolidation: start with account reconciliation automation instead. Firms that automate reconciliations close faster.33
