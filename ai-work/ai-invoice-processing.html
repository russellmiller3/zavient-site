Accounts payable automation: what does it cost, what needs a person?

Bottom line
  • The money leaks in exceptions: about 1 invoice in 5 needs a person.8,1
  • Buy an AP suite such as BILL, Ramp or Tipalti to run approvals and payments.5,6

How is AI accounts payable automation different from OCR?

OCR (optical character recognition) only turns an invoice into text. AI AP automation also codes it, matches it to the purchase order (PO) and routes it for approval.27,21 Reading costs cents,10,11 but money leaks in two places: exceptions, about 1 invoice in 5,8 and fake bank-change requests.12

Where does your team stay in charge?

Your AP team stays in charge at four points: clearing exceptions, approving supplier bank-detail changes, releasing payments and owning fraud checks.8,49,47,51 The tool flags; a person makes the call.8

  • AP specialist: clears the exceptions, about 1 invoice in 5, and makes the call the tool only flags.8,1 Keep three-way match (invoice, PO and receipt agree) on; Washington's state auditor says it "should not be eliminated in order to save time."48
  • Supplier-list owner, not the paying clerk: adds suppliers and approves any bank-detail change.47,49 Each change gets a call-back on a number already on file, never one from the request.49,12
  • AP manager or treasury: releases payment, and is never the person who entered the invoice.47,48 Federal standards split approving, recording and paying so no one person controls a payment; small teams can use other checks instead.50,47
  • Controller: owns fraud monitoring on ACH (US bank-transfer) payments. Since June 2026, Nacha, which runs ACH, requires every business that sends ACH payments to have risk-based fraud checks, covering vendor impersonation.51,52

What does your team do after?

After AI takes over reading and coding, your AP team spends its time on exceptions, supplier questions, fraud checks and analysis.27,1,55,53

  • Exceptions become the main job. Ardent calls them the biggest single reason AP costs are not lower, and they hit about 1 invoice in 5.1,8
  • Fewer "where's my payment?" calls: AP staff spend 21.9% of their time dealing with suppliers (Ardent) and 29.4% on supplier inquiries (Bottomline 2026).1,55 Ardent's top-performing teams spend about half as much time on supplier inquiries, and exceptions cause much of it.1
  • Fraud checks: most AP teams already help prevent fraud; 72% of AP departments reported at least moderate involvement in 2026, and 79% in 2025.55,54 Ardent calls AP "often the last line of defense against fraud."54
  • Analysis: BLS expects clerks to take on "a more analytical and advisory role" as software automates routine tasks.53 Among US firms using AI, 66% use it only to help staff; 2% saw AI-related employment fall.56

How does AI move an invoice from email to your ERP?

AI reads the emailed invoice, pulls out the supplier, lines and totals, matches them to the purchase order and receipt, codes them, and posts a draft bill to your ERP.20,25,34 Anything off, like new bank details, waits for a person to call the supplier on a number already on file.12,49

Where do you start automating accounts payable?

Most small and mid-size companies should start by buying an AP suite and switching on its AI.5,6,7 Add your own AI check only where exceptions or fraud still get through; use capture alone if your ERP already runs approvals and payment.8,10

1. Buy the suite and switch on its AI

Most small and mid-size companies.

  1. Suppliers email invoices to the AP inbox
  2. Suite reads and codes them; AP clerk checks the flagged ones
  3. Suite matches each invoice to the PO and the receipt that Receiving logged
  4. Budget owner approves; Controller approves over limit
  5. AP manager or treasury releases payment; the approver can't release it
  6. Suite syncs to your accounting system (ERP)

Cost: BILL costs $49 to $89 per user a month.5 Ramp is free, or $15 per user a month plus a platform fee;6 Tipalti starts at $99 a month plus fees.7

2. Add an AI check where your suite falls short

You already have AP software but exceptions and fraud attempts still get through.

  1. New invoice lands in the AP inbox
  2. AI reads it and shows where on the page each value came from
  3. AI compares it with the PO, the receipt that Receiving logged and the supplier record
  4. AI flags any bank-detail change and holds the payment
  5. The person who owns the supplier list, not the paying clerk, calls the supplier back on a number already on file.49,12
  6. AP clerk releases it to approval; Controller approves over limit

Cost: Reading an invoice costs cents with a cloud invoice reader,10,11 against a median $6.00 to an average $9.90 to process one invoice today.2,8 The real cost is building and owning the hold rules; we quote that per job and don't publish a price here.

3. Capture only, into your own ERP

High volume of standard invoices and an ERP that already handles approvals and payment.

  1. AP clerk scans paper invoices or forwards the PDFs
  2. Capture tool or cloud invoice parser reads them
  3. If AI is unsure, it reads again
  4. AP clerk checks what is still unsure
  5. AP clerk posts to the ERP

Cost: Amazon Textract reads invoices for $10 per 1,000 pages;10 Google charges $0.10 per invoice of up to 10 pages;11 Rossum starts at $18,000 a year.29

Which e-invoicing and tax rules apply?

Germany and France now require B2B e-invoices, and the US has no mandate.36,38,42 France made large and mid-size firms issue them from 1 Sep 2026; Germany's larger firms must from 1 Jan 2027.38,36

France: B2B e-invoicingStarted 1 Sep 2026Every business must be able to receive e-invoices; large and mid-size firms must issue them. Small firms issue from 1 Sep 2027.38,39 A scan or an ordinary PDF sent by email no longer counts as an e-invoice.40

How do you benchmark an AP tool on your own invoices?

Run each tool on 50 of your own recent invoices, scored against answers you wrote down first, and count wrong values, missed bank-detail changes and missed duplicates.9,60 Record cost per invoice and exception rate beforehand, so you can measure the saving.8

  1. Record your before numbers first: cost per invoice, exception rate, straight-through rate and days per invoice. Multiply cost per invoice by your monthly volume to get your unit's AP cost today.
  2. Compare your baseline with the benchmark figures on this page: 19.9% exceptions, 38.3% straight through and 11.6 days per invoice.8
  3. For two weeks, log the minutes your team spends on each exception. That gives you your own cost of an exception.
  4. Pull 50 recent invoices from one entity or one AP inbox, including at least 10 past exceptions. Include credit notes, multi-page invoices, foreign ones and the worst scans.
  5. Write down the right supplier, number, date, total, tax and PO for each before any tool reads them.
  6. Run each tool on the same set. Count wrong values apart from blanks: a wrong total is worse than a missing one.
  7. Add three invoices that change a supplier's bank details, and check each tool holds them.
  8. Add one invoice you already paid, resubmitted, and check each tool flags it as a duplicate.
  9. Measure how many invoices go straight through, how many reach your team as exceptions, and the cost per invoice including add-ons. Multiply the change in cost per invoice by your monthly volume to get the saving in dollars.

Where does accounts payable automation go wrong?

Accounts payable automation goes wrong in three places: add-ons that raise the price, fake bank-detail changes riding on invoices, and tools that read the subtotal as the total.5,16,12,9

  • The price you see is rarely the price you pay. Matching, payments, more entities and ERP sync are often add-ons.5,16,7,23
  • Payment fraud rides on invoices:12,13 74% of US organizations experienced business email compromise in 2025, the AFP found.4,15 Check any change to bank details by calling the supplier on a number already on file.49,12
  • A tool that reads the subtotal as the total looks right at a glance. In our benchmark, 7 of 10 tools picked the wrong one of two look-alike tables.9

Questions buyers ask about accounts payable automation

What is accounts payable automation?

Software that reads supplier invoices, codes them, matches them to the purchase order and routes them for approval and payment.19,27 AI does the reading and suggests the coding; your AP team still clears exceptions and approves payments.8,47

How much does AP automation cost?

Listed AP automation prices start at free (Ramp), $49 per user a month (BILL) and $99 a month (Tipalti).6,5,7 Those are the only 3 of the 9 full AP suites we checked that publish a price; the rest quote after a demo.18,19 Add-ons such as matching and payment fees cost extra.16,17

Will AI replace accounts payable?

No, AI changes the accounts payable job more than it removes it. Among US firms using AI, 66% use it only to help staff, and 2% saw AI-related employment fall.56 BLS still projects bookkeeping and accounting clerk jobs to fall 6% by 2035, with about 144,100 openings a year.53

How long does AP automation take to set up?

Vendors say weeks, not months: AvidXchange says many customers are live in as little as six weeks; Ramp says 2 to 12.19,57 Ramp says most of that time goes to cleaning supplier data and settling who approves what.57 Tipalti says technical setup takes days, but company checks and account setup can take several weeks.58

Can I trust AP automation accuracy claims?

Only after a test on your own invoices.60 In a Fraunhofer study, the best model got 96.50% of fields right on clean invoices but 87.46% on scanned receipts.59 In our own test, two look-alike tables fooled 7 of 10 tools.9

Should you buy an AP suite or build your own checks?

Buy an AP suite for the workflow, and build only the checks it misses, such as fraud holds and duplicate-invoice checks.5,6,7,22

Buy: Buy for the workflow: inbox, approvals, payments, ERP sync and an audit trail. Suites already sell all of this,5,6,7 and you do not want to own payments.

Build: Build only the checks your suite misses: exception triage, fraud holds, duplicate-invoice checks,22 odd document types or your own coding rules. Start with one entity or one AP inbox, and keep the rest on today's process until the benchmark holds. Exceptions still hit about one invoice in five,8 so these checks flag what your AP team should look at; the decision stays with a person.

When is an AP suite the wrong fit?

An AP suite is the wrong fit when your accounting software's built-in bill capture already handles simple bills, or when you need extracted data rather than a workflow.33,34,29,10 Many entities or currencies call for a global suite instead.7,21,24,23

  • Already on QuickBooks or Xero with simple bills: use the bill capture built into your accounting software first.33,34
  • Many entities, countries or currencies: look at the global suites (Tipalti, Coupa, Basware, Medius). Expect a contract and no list price.7,21,24,23
  • Need data, not workflow? A capture tool or cloud parser feeds your accounting system (ERP) directly.29,10
  • You run Microsoft Power Platform: AI Builder has a prebuilt invoice model, limited to 360 calls a minute and 20 MB per file.35
