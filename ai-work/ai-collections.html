What is AI for collections, and what does it change?

AI for collections is software that ranks overdue loans, picks when and how to reach each borrower, and drafts the message.10,2 What changes: collectors stop dialling cold and spend their day on hardship cases, high balances and escalations.1,2 Contact limits, consent and hand-offs to people must be built in.11,12

Which collections decisions stay with your people?

Your collectors and managers keep every decision that changes what a borrower owes or stops contact: bankruptcy and military matches, hardship plans, disputes and message templates.13,14,16

  • Your people keep every decision that changes what a borrower owes or whether contact must stop. AI only ranks, times and drafts.12,13
  • Collections manager: confirms every bankruptcy or military-service match before anyone makes contact. A bankruptcy filing stops collection, and the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (SCRA) caps debt taken on before service at 6% interest.14,15
  • Senior collector or loan officer: decides hardship plans, loan modifications and disputes. A modification counts as new credit under fair-lending rules, and credit-report disputes need an investigation.13,16
  • Compliance lead: approves every template and the house contact cap, your own limit per account, and samples AI calls and texts weekly. Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) examiners found over 100 calls made after people asked a collector to stop.17,18

What do collectors do once AI takes the first pass?

Collectors spend their day on hardship cases, high balances, escalations and borrowers AI could not reach, instead of dialling cold.1,2

  • Collectors spend the day on accounts that need a person: hardship, high balances and borrowers AI could not reach. When a person took over on day 6, AI's shortfall fell to 0.8 points.1,3
  • Collectors work escalations instead of dialling cold. One credit union reported 4% of AI conversations went to a person, mostly members who asked for one (vendor webinar).2
  • New collectors get up to speed faster: AI help raised support agents' output 14%, and 34% for novices.19,20 The Bureau of Labor Statistics expects about 11,200 collector openings a year to 2035.21
  • Among US firms where AI changed tasks, 66% use it only to help staff and 2% report employment decreases.22,23

What does AI do with one overdue loan?

On a 34-day-late auto loan, AI ranks it as likely to cure, schedules a 6:30pm text with a payment link, and holds the draft for a collector's approval.24,11

Which way to build AI for collections fits your firm?

Most firms should turn on the AI in the collections system they already run. Add usage-priced reminders for many small early accounts, and build only with a data team.27,8,42

1. Fits most firms (1-4 collectors): turn on the AI in the collections system you run

For a credit union or bank already on AKUVO, Temenos, C&R Software or a core collections module.27,29 You get ranking and drafting without a new system to vet.43

  1. Collections managerList today's queues, cure rates and roll rates
  2. IT leadSwitch on the vendor's AI module for one test queue
  3. Compliance leadApprove message templates and the house contact cap
  4. CollectorWork the AI-ranked queue and flag bad picks
  5. Collections managerCompare cures with the old queue after 60 days

Cost: Not published by AKUVO, Temenos or C&R Software, so ask for a quote on your account volume.28,29,31 Budget time for the vendor review bank regulators expect.43

2. Many small early-stage accounts (5-15 collectors): add usage-priced reminders and AI voice

For lenders with many small balances in the first 30 days late, where a timely reminder often cures the loan.8,9

  1. Data analystScore each account for its chance to cure on its own
  2. Compliance leadSet consent rules, quiet hours and contact caps for each state
  3. Operations leadSend texts and AI-voice reminders only where consent is on file
  4. CollectorTake every hand-off and every account still late by day 6 to 10
  5. Quality reviewerCheck a sample of AI calls and texts each week

Cost: Illustrative: 340 accounts × 3 calls × 3 minutes × $0.11 a minute is about $340 a month in voice.26 Texts cost $0.0083 a segment plus carrier fees, or pay on results at 1.5% of recovered plus $1 a payment.35,6

3. Data team and 3+ years of history: build your own ranking and drafting

For lenders whose own payment history is the edge and who already have an analyst. Published studies show this ranking works when tested against the current strategy.42,44

  1. AnalystBuild a model of who will pay and the best time to reach them
  2. Model risk reviewerValidate it under your model risk policy
  3. Collections managerRun it against today's strategy on a random half of accounts
  4. CollectorApprove each AI-drafted message before it goes out
  5. Vendor managerReview any voice or language-model supplier as a third party

Cost: Your analysts' time plus usage costs like recipe 2.26,35 No source we found prices an in-house build.

Which rules apply to AI for collections?

A lender collecting its own loans faces unfair-practice law (UDAAP), the TCPA for calls and texts, and state collection laws. Regulation F mainly binds outside collectors.5,48,4 Bankruptcy, military, fair-lending and credit-dispute rules apply either way.14,15

How do you test AI for collections before you trust it?

Run the AI on a random share of accounts and today's strategy on the rest, then compare cures, roll rates, complaints and opt-outs after 90 days.10,42,36

  1. Test it side by side: AI on a random share of accounts, today's strategy on the rest, compared after 90 days.10,42
  2. Pull your last 90 days of numbers from your system, so there is no wait. Track roll rate (share moving from 30 to 60 days late), cures, promises kept, complaints and opt-outs.36,37
  3. Pilot one product, such as auto loans, in its first 30 days late. AI's shortfall in the 22-million-case study peaked about a month in.1
  4. Write the hand-off rule before launch: any account not cured by day 6 to 10 goes to a collector.1
  5. Count every call, text and email against one house cap. Regulators judge total contact, not one channel.11,17
  6. Script 20 or more ways people say stop, bankruptcy, deployed or not my debt. Check each one stops contact or reaches a person.17,65
  7. Sample AI calls and drafted messages weekly for false or threatening statements.18,53
  8. Price the lift: extra cures × average balance × your charge-off rate, minus usage cost. The credit union average charge-off rate is 0.78%.36,26
  9. Treat rising complaints or opt-outs as a stop signal, even if recoveries rise.66,17

Where does AI for collections go wrong?

AI for collections goes wrong when it deals with borrowers alone: AI-only calls collect less, and missing consent, stacked contacts, threatening drafts and missed refusals bring legal risk.1,17,48,18

  • It goes wrong where AI deals with borrowers alone: AI-only calls, missing consent, stacked contacts, threatening drafts and missed refusals.1,17
  • AI-only calling collects less: 9% less at 30 days and 21% fewer promises to pay. Hand off to people early.1,3
  • AI voices without consent: the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) treats them as artificial voices, at $500 a call, tripled if willful. Check consent per number and the reassigned-numbers database first.48,56,67
  • Contacts stacking across channels: Massachusetts allows creditors 2 calls or texts a week per debt. Run one counter across calls, texts and emails, set to your strictest state.60,11
  • Drafts that threaten: Navy Federal's letters implied legal action and cost about $23M in refunds plus a $5.5M penalty. Have compliance approve every template.18,53
  • Bots that trap people or miss a refusal: the CFPB warns chatbots that block human help can break the law. Test a one-step route to a person monthly.12,65

Questions buyers ask about AI for collections

Does Regulation F apply when a bank or credit union collects its own loans?

Generally no: it excludes a creditor's own staff collecting in the creditor's name.4,49 Unfair-practice law, the TCPA and state laws still apply, and many lenders copy Regulation F's limits anyway.5,50

Can AI replace human debt collectors?

Not on current evidence: in 22 million cases, AI callers collected less than people, and handing off early closed most of the gap.1,3

Does AI reduce collector workload?

Yes, on routine reminders and first contacts. One credit union reported only 4% of AI conversations needed a person (vendor webinar).2 Plain email and text reminders also cut late payment.8,9

Do we need consent to use an AI voice?

Yes, for calls to cell phones. The FCC treats AI voices as artificial voices under the TCPA.48,55 Some credit unions add consent language to the membership agreement (vendor webinar).2

How are AI collections tools priced?

AI collections tools charge by monthly voice minutes ($99 to $4,999), by a share of what they recover (1.5%), or per call minute or text.7,6,26,35 Voice building blocks run $0.07 to $0.31 a minute, and texts about $0.0083 each.26,35 Nine of the 11 vendors we checked publish no price.6,7

Who handles hardship and disputes when AI is in the loop?

A person, every time, because a loan modification counts as new credit under fair-lending rules and credit-report disputes need an investigation.13,16

Should you buy AI for collections or build it?

Buy: Usually buy first, when your collections system offers an AI module, you have no data team and your strategy is standard.27,29 Most enterprise AI spend now goes to bought apps.68 A bought tool still needs a third-party risk review.43

Build: Build the ranking and rules layer when your payment history is the edge or you collect across states with different caps.60,62 Bought tools from specialist vendors succeed more often than internal builds, so build only with a named owner.69

When should a collections team pick something else?

Pick something else when your collections system already has AI built in, the debt is charged off and belongs with an agency, or a plain reminder will do.27,4,8

  • Pick something else when your system already has AI built in, the debt belongs with an agency, or a plain reminder will do.27,8
  • Already on AKUVO, Temenos or C&R Software: try that platform's own AI module before adding a new vendor.27,29,31
  • Charged-off debt past your own recovery stage: a licensed collection agency such as TrueAccord, which must follow Regulation F.4,47
  • Early, low-balance accounts: autopay enrolment and plain text or email reminders, which cut late payment without AI.8,9
