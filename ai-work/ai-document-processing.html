How does AI document processing work, and how is it different from OCR?

AI document processing reads a page's text like OCR does, then labels which value is which, such as the total or due date3,4. Plain OCR (text recognition) stops at raw text2,3. Start with the document your team re-keys most: reading costs cents, and staff time is the real cost5,1.

Where does your team stay in charge?

Your team keeps three calls: a reviewer settles facts the models disagree on, a team lead sets the acceptable error rate, and a separate approver releases the facts5,23,24. Any reviewer can override any AI value17,23.

  • Reviewer, someone who knows the documents: settles every fact the two models disagree on.5 The reviewer can override any AI value.17,23
  • Team lead: sets the error rate the team will accept and signs off before go-live. NIST says people, not the tool, should set those limits.23
  • Approver, not the person who fixed the value: releases facts that feed money or reports into the system others rely on.24 Small teams that can't split duties can use other checks.24
  • Every reviewer checks the quoted page, not just the number.31 The EU AI Act names the risk of leaning on AI output: automation bias.17

What does your team do after?

After AI reads the documents, your team checks instead of typing and settles the facts the two models disagree on, about 1 in 4 in our test26,5. Staff also get time back for harder cases, about 2.2 hours a week for generative AI users28.

  • Checking replaces typing. Checking was already part of the job: O*NET lists comparing data with source documents as a core data-entry task.26
  • Disagreements become the main work: in our test, about 1 fact in 4 went to a person.5
  • New staff get up to speed faster. In a study of 5,172 support agents, those two months in with AI help matched agents six months in without it.27
  • Time back for harder cases: workers who use generative AI report saving 5.4% of their hours, about 2.2 hours a week.28 Among US firms using AI, 66% use it only to help staff.25

What does AI pull out of a fund report?

AI pulls out the fund's commitment, paid-in capital, distributions and NAV (net asset value), each with its page number and the exact words it came from5.

Should a person check every AI-read field, or only the exceptions?

A person should check only the exceptions: the facts whose quote fails the code check or where two models disagree, about 1 fact in 4 in our benchmark5.

Cheap and fast: one model, quotes checked by code

Internal use, modest volume, a person already reviews the output.

  1. PDF
  2. Small model reads it
  3. Each fact + page + quote
  4. Code checks the quote is on that page
  5. Your reviewer fixes failures and logs each fix

Cost: Under 2 cents per 57-page report with OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna, Anthropic's Claude Haiku 5.5 or DeepSeek V4.1 Flash in our hard round5. Add review time.

Fewest wrong numbers: two models, a person on disagreements

The numbers feed money decisions, reports to investors or a regulator.

  1. PDF
  2. Model A and Model B read it
  3. Code compares fact by fact
  4. AgreeAccept
  5. DisagreeYour reviewer decides, and a second person approves (maker-checker)
  6. System logs each decision and who made it
  7. Approved facts load to your accounting, CRM (customer relationship management) or reporting system

Cost: About $0.27 per report for two top models in our locked-answer round5. The two models disagreed on 66 of 270 facts across all 30 reports, about 1 in 4, and a person settled each5.

High volume, standard forms: cloud parser first, model for the hard cases

Many invoices, receipts or IDs in layouts the clouds already know.

  1. Scan or PDF
  2. Cloud prebuilt parser
  3. Low confidence?
  4. Model re-reads it
  5. Person on what is still unsure

Cost: About a penny a page for prebuilt parsers at AWS and Azure2,4, plus model cost on the share they cannot read.

Which rules apply when AI reads financial documents?

Three rules can apply: the EU AI Act for credit or insurance pricing, US Regulation B for credit declines, and New York Department of Financial Services (DFS) insurer guidance15,20,22. Reading documents is not high-risk on its own; the rules bite when the facts feed those decisions15.

RuleStatusWhat it means for you
EU AI Act, Annex IIIStarts 2 Dec 2027Reading documents is not a high-risk use on its own. It becomes part of one when the facts feed credit scoring of people or life and health insurance pricing15. Then logging and human oversight apply16,17, from 2 December 202718,19.
Regulation B, 12 CFR 1002.9In forceIf extracted facts feed a US credit decision, a decline still needs specific reasons20, even when an algorithm made it20,33. The CFPB withdrew its 2022 circular on algorithm declines in May 2025, but the rule itself still applies33,21. A wrongly read fact becomes a wrong reason, so keep the page and quote behind every fact.
NYDFS Circular Letter No. 7In force since Jul 2024New York insurers that use AI in underwriting and pricing stay responsible for vendor tools22. Where available, their vendor contracts should give them audit rights22.

Not legal advice. Check with your counsel.

How do you benchmark AI document tools on your own files?

Lock the right answers for 50 of your own files first, run each tool on them, make every answer quote its page, and count wrong values apart from blanks14,11.

  1. Record today's baseline first: staff minutes per file, and errors that reach the systems and reports downstream.
  2. Pick 50 real files from one team's queue, including your worst scans and longest reports, plus facts some files never state, to catch invented answers. Even vendors advise benchmarking on your own documents14.
  3. Write the right answer for every fact before any AI reads the files, and lock it, so the AI's output cannot sway it.
  4. Require each answer to name its page and quote the exact words, and have code check that the quote is there.
  5. Count wrong answers apart from blanks. A blank goes to a person; a wrong number can slip through.
  6. Report minutes, wrong fields and cost per file, before and after; multiply the change by monthly volume to price it in dollars. Run the winner again on new files before you go live.

Can you trust a vendor's accuracy numbers?

Not on their own: most vendor accuracy numbers come from the vendor's own benchmark runs, so test on your own files14. Public answer keys have errors too12.

  • Vendor accuracy numbers mostly come from the vendor's own benchmark runs14, and public answer keys have errors too12. Even on our locked-answer set, when the AI and our key differed, the AI was right in 22 of 57 cases, so check your key5.
  • A model that writes a plausible wrong number is worse than one that leaves the field blank11. Score them separately.
  • Two tables on one page with similar labels fooled 7 of 10 tools in our hard round5. Check the facts that sit near look-alikes.
  • Prices and model versions change: Mistral's newer OCR model costs twice its older one6. Re-run your benchmark when either changes, and keep the files and answers so the re-run is cheap.
  • Read the vendor's data terms. Datalab's rate card shows prices with a 25% discount that assumes you let it train on your data7.
  • Plan reviewer time: in our benchmark, about 1 fact in 4 (66 of 270) went to a person5. Multiply by the facts you pull each month to size the workload.

Questions buyers ask about AI document processing

What is the difference between OCR and AI document processing?

OCR turns a page into raw text, while document AI also knows which number is which, like the total or the due date.2,3,4 Use OCR alone when you only need searchable text.2

How accurate is AI document processing?

AI document processing is very accurate on clean files: on our 30 fund reports, the two best models made no mistakes.5 Accuracy still depends on your documents, so test on your own.23,14 Zero in 30 still allows a true rate of up to about 1 in 10.13

Can AI read scanned documents?

Yes, but accuracy drops on scans. In a 2025 study, the best model got 96.5% of invoice fields right on clean files and 92.7% on scanned ones.29 Re-run on 10 of our reports as scanned images, both top tools still made no mistakes.5

Is it safe to send company documents to an AI model?

Only under business or API (application programming interface) terms you have read: OpenAI and Anthropic say they do not train on that data by default.30,31 OpenAI may keep API data up to 30 days to spot abuse, unless you qualify for zero retention.30

Will AI document processing replace data entry staff?

Not entirely: AI changes the job from typing to checking, though fewer keying jobs are projected.26,32 Among US firms using AI, 66% use it only to help staff, and AI-related employment fell in 2%.25 BLS still projects data entry keyer jobs to fall 25.5% by 2035.32

Should you buy a document parser or build your own?

Buy a prebuilt parser for standard invoices, receipts or IDs, and build on frontier models when your files are unique and a wrong number is expensive2,4,5.

Buy: Buy a prebuilt parser or an accounts payable tool when your files are standard invoices, receipts or IDs. The big clouds' prebuilt models already cover those2,4. Buying fits best when you mainly need volume and an audit trail.

Build: Build on frontier models (the newest, most capable ones) when your files are unique to your business and a wrong number is expensive. The build is a prompt, a fact list, a checker and a review queue where your team settles about 2 facts per report5. If files must stay in-house, run an open-source model on your own servers; in our benchmark, local models made more wrong values5.

When should you pick something else?

Pick something other than a frontier model when you read thousands of standard invoices a day, when files cannot leave your network, or when searchable text is enough2,4,5,3. Use a prebuilt cloud parser, a local open-source model or plain OCR, respectively2,5,3.

  • Thousands of standard invoices a day? A prebuilt cloud parser, a ready-made reader for standard forms, costs about a penny a page2,4.
  • Your files cannot leave your network: run an open-source model on your own servers. Expect more wrong values: in our first round the two local models made 8 each, where Anthropic's Claude Haiku 5.5 made none5.
  • You only need searchable text, not fields: basic text recognition (OCR) costs well under a penny a page at all three big clouds2,3,4.
