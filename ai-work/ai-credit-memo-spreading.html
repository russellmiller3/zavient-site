What is financial spreading, and what can AI change in the credit memo?

Financial spreading is keying each line of a borrower's returns and statements into one template, so ratios compare across years and borrowers.40,32 The credit memo is the write-up that documents the lending decision.1,2 AI now takes the first pass at both, and checking its numbers decides whether it works.21,28

Where do your credit analysts and officers stay in charge?

Your credit officer still decides and signs, analysts check every flagged figure against its source page, and loan review stays independent of approval.1,28,9

  • Credit officer: makes and signs the lending decision. The file must still show repayment ability and the borrower's financial condition, whoever drafted it.1,30
  • Credit analyst: checks every flagged figure against its source page and edits the narrative.28,29 In TD's reported 2026 test, 95% of evaluated outputs passed expert review, so 1 in 20 did not.17
  • Loan review: stays independent of origination and approval, and samples AI-drafted files after go-live.9,10 Whoever checks drafts inside the approval chain isn't that reviewer.
  • COO or vendor owner: validates the vendor tool and tracks its results over time.5 Using a vendor doesn't reduce the bank's responsibility.11

What do credit analysts do once AI takes the first pass?

Credit analysts move from keying numbers to clearing flagged figures, cross-checking documents for fraud and spending more time on risk and clients.17,14,18

  • Analysts move from keying lines to clearing flags and testing the credit. At one credit union, a single analyst cut spreading to 30-45 minutes as commercial loans grew 25% (vendor case).41
  • Credit staff get more time for risk and clients: TD says that is what Credit Assist frees.17 NAB reports bankers moving freed time to customers.18
  • Freed hours go to fraud checks: FinCEN warns of AI-made fake identity documents that show up as inconsistencies between them.14 IRS transcripts confirm the returns you spread.15
  • Headcount isn't the goal: of US firms using AI, 66% only use it to augment tasks.16

What does an AI-drafted spread and credit memo look like?

Borrower returns, an interim statement and guarantor files go in, and a cited spread, a flag list and a draft memo come out for the analyst.28,30

Goes inComes out
Illustrative: a real estate loan to a 60-person manufacturer. The file holds three years of business returns, an interim statement, two guarantors' personal returns and a debt schedule. By hand, the spread takes 2-4 hours and the memo 4-8 more, in vendor estimates.33,30A spread with each line linked to its page, subtotals recalculated, and a flag list of unmapped lines and totals that don't tie.28,38 A draft memo with debt service coverage, cash flow across the business and guarantors, and risks, each figure cited.30 The analyst clears the flags and edits, and the credit officer decides.1

How accurate is AI at reading financial documents?

General AI models are not accurate enough to use unchecked: about two in three table-reading tests failed in one study.22,23 These studies test general models, not tuned spreading products.

Which way to start fits your lender?

Start with a self-serve tool if deals are few, your loan system's module if you already run one, or a custom build for unusual borrowers.35,26,47

1. Small lender, a few commercial deals a month

One to three credit analysts on a loan system with no spreading built in. 1st Community Federal Credit Union ran commercial lending with one credit analyst.41

  1. Loan processorCollect the returns and the borrower's signed IRS Form 4506-C, the consent for tax transcripts
  2. AnalystUpload the package to a self-serve spreading tool
  3. AIRead each line, map it to your template and recalculate subtotals
  4. AnalystClear every flag and compare key lines with the IRS transcript
  5. AnalystEdit the AI-drafted memo narrative in your template
  6. Credit officerApprove or decline

Cost: LenderAnalyzer lists $99 to $399 a month and Floowed from $100 a month, read 26 Nov 2026.35,37 IRS transcripts cost $4 each.15

2. Mid-size lender already on nCino, Abrigo or Moody's

Steady commercial volume and several analysts on a loan system that sells its own spreading and memo module.26,32

  1. COOTurn on the module, with contract notice of new AI features and limits on training with your data8
  2. Analyst leadRun the test steps below on 20-50 files you already approved
  3. AISpread each package and draft the memo
  4. AnalystsWork the flag queue and edit each draft
  5. Credit officerApprove or decline
  6. Loan reviewSample AI-drafted files each quarter9

Cost: nCino, Abrigo and Moody's publish no price.26,32,44 Dieterich Bank set up nCino's spreading itself in 8 weeks in 2023 (vendor case).42

3. Private credit or document-heavy lender with its own template

A custom spread template, borrowers with several entities, and someone in-house who handles data. Cloud document reading is priced by the page.47

  1. Data leadSet up document reading into your own spread template
  2. AIDraft memo sections with a page citation on every figure
  3. AnalystCheck every number outside the error margin you set
  4. Credit officerApprove or decline
  5. Model ownerLog the tool under your model governance and track its results5

How do you test AI spreading on your own files?

Run 20-50 files your analysts already signed through the tool, score each step against a double-keyed answer key, and trace every memo figure to a page.40,25

  1. Record today's numbers on your last 20-50 commercial files: hours per spread, hours per memo and errors your second reviewer caught. Keying error rates vary widely, so you need your own.24
  2. Pilot with one lending team on files it already spread and your credit officer signed, so a right answer exists. TD tested on 220 curated deals.17
  3. Build the answer key by double entry: two people key the key lines and a third settles differences. Double entry beats checking by eye.25,24
  4. Score extraction, mapping and calculation separately, plus whether each figure traces to its page.40,21
  5. Test your hardest documents on their own: scanned partner income schedules (K-1s), multi-entity returns and handwritten interim statements. Accuracy falls as tasks get harder.23
  6. Count every figure in the draft memo with no source page. The target is zero.13,39
  7. Pull IRS transcripts for a sample and compare them with the returns the AI read.15
  8. Price the lift: hours saved per spread and memo, times files a year, plus how many more deals each analyst can take.17,18
  9. Decide with your chief credit officer to keep, change or stop, and have loan review sample AI-drafted files after go-live.9

Where do AI spreads and memos go wrong?

AI spreads and memos go wrong through confident wrong numbers, untested accuracy claims, fake documents and rubber-stamped drafts.22,14,20

  • Confident wrong numbers: general chatbots got about two in three table tests wrong, and NIST names this risk confabulation.22,13 Recalculate totals and require a source page for every figure.38
  • Accuracy claims with no denominator: none of the 12 vendors we checked says how many documents its figure covers.27,28 Ask for the test set, then run your own.
  • Fake or altered documents: FinCEN warns of AI-made fake identity documents, flagged by inconsistencies between them.14 Check returns against IRS transcripts.15
  • Rubber-stamping: in a 2026 field experiment, targeted human disagreement with the algorithm improved accuracy, but excessive overrides hurt.20 Have analysts sign specific checks, and keep loan review independent.9
  • A vendor quietly adds AI features or trains on your data: put notice terms and data-use limits in the contract.8,11

Questions lenders ask about financial spreading and credit memos

What is financial spreading?

Financial spreading is keying each line of a borrower's tax returns and statements into one standard template.40,32 Ratios and cash flow then compare across years and borrowers.40

How long does it take to spread financial statements?

By hand, a spread takes 30 minutes to 4 hours depending on the file, in vendor estimates.36,33 One credit union cut it from 3-4 hours to 30-45 minutes with automation (vendor case).41

Can AI write a credit memo?

Yes, AI can write a cited first draft, but a person reviews it and makes the credit decision.17,30 In TD's 2026 test, 95% of evaluated outputs passed expert review on 220 deals.17

What does financial spreading software cost?

Published prices start at $99 a month (LenderAnalyzer) and $100 a month (Floowed), read 26 Nov 2026.35,37 nCino, Abrigo and Moody's publish no price.26,32,44

How accurate is AI financial spreading?

Vendors claim 95% to over 99%, but none we checked says how many documents those figures were measured on.28,27 Test it on files your analysts already signed.

What should a credit memo include?

A credit memo should show the borrower's repayment ability, financial condition and guarantor support, so the lending decision is informed.1 SBA requires one with every 7(a) application.4

Should you buy spreading software or build your own?

Buy spreading software if your template is standard, and build only when your own template and borrower mix are the edge.48,49

Buy: Buy if your spread template is standard, your loan system sells a module and you have no data team. Most US enterprises buy their AI tools, and vendor-built or partnered tools reach daily use about twice as often as internal builds.48,51 Vendors and cases put go-live at 2-8 weeks.30,42

Build: Build if your own template and credit policy are the edge, or your borrowers have many entities. Cloud document reading costs $10 per 1,000 pages, but validation stays with you.47,5

When should you pick something else?

Pick something else if you close few deals, mostly spread simple returns, already own a loan system module, or need peer ratios more than faster keying.25,32,50

  • Only a handful of commercial deals a year: stay in Excel and double-key the key lines. Double entry beats checking by eye.25,24
  • Mostly simple tax returns: your loan system's own auto-spreading plus IRS transcripts may be enough.32,15
  • Already on nCino, Abrigo or Moody's: turn on its own spreading and memo module before buying another tool.26,32,43
  • Peer ratios are the gap, not keying: ProSight Statement Studies give industry ratios for 750+ industries, from statements its member institutions contribute.50
