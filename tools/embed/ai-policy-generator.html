How the generator works
  • Every policy gets 18 sections: the parts the main frameworks ask for, from scope and owner to training, incidents and review.[^nist_600][^iso42001][^nist_rmf]
  • Your answers switch clauses on. Health data adds the business associate rule, hiring adds bias checks, and so on. Each added clause carries its source in the policy's notes.
  • State and EU clauses come from the law's own text or the regulator's page where we could read it, checked on 26 Nov 2026.[^co_sb189][^nyc_aedt][^eu_lit]
  • The risk check sorts each AI use into Low, Medium, High or Not allowed. It follows the EU AI Act's risk levels and NIST's advice to match review to impact.[^eu_act][^nist_rmf]
  • Defaults we chose ourselves are marked "Zavient estimate": the 5-day answer time, the 24-hour report window and the yearly training refresher. Change them in the policy.
  • Your answers and edits stay in your browser. We don't see them, and there is no sign-up.
