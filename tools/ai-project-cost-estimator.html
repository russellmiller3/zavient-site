AI agent cost estimator: what will an AI agent cost to run?

Bottom line
  • Most of the cost is people's time: set-up, training and checking the AI's work, not the model15,17.
  • Ready-made support agents charge about $1 to $2 for each conversation they handle8,10.
  • Turning on AI in software you already pay for costs $20 to $30 a person a month6,5.
  • Model fees are rarely the biggest line: about $690 a month for 10,000 documents on a mid-tier model1,3.
  • Messy data breaks budgets: Gartner expects firms to abandon 60% of AI projects without AI-ready data22,21.

How it works

  • Model fees = items × tokens per item × list price per million tokens × 1.15 for retries3,1,2. Low, expected and high use small, mid and top-tier models.
  • Review time = items × share checked × minutes per check × $46.89 an hour, US pay plus benefits13,16. The share checked follows how bad a mistake is, a Zavient estimate.
  • Set-up uses published effort bands: 200 to 1,400 build hours and 3 to 5 days per connected system12,11. Outside work is priced at federal (GSA) schedule rates for AI work28.
  • Seat and per-conversation fees are list prices checked 26 Nov 2026 that still apply after 31 Dec 20264,5,8,10. Promotions are left out.
  • Training is 10 hours per person, and upkeep on a custom build is 15% to 25% of the build a year14,18,19. Lines marked Zavient estimate are our judgment around a published anchor.

What does an AI agent cost, and what drives it?

Running an AI agent costs cents to about $2 an item, or $20 to $30 a person a month in seats1,8,5. Volume drives the usage bill, but people's time drives most of the cost: set-up, connecting systems and checking the agent's work21,15,11,17.

Where does your team stay in charge?

The ops lead decides which items the agent finishes alone, the people who do the work check its output, and the budget owner caps monthly usage fees16,17,21.

  • The ops lead who owns the workflow decides which items the agent may finish alone and which a person checks16.
  • The people who do the work today check the agent's output and handle the exceptions. Budget that time as its own line17.
  • The budget owner sets a monthly cap on usage fees, because they grow with volume and can sit on top of seat fees21,27.

What do buyers ask about AI agent costs?

Buyers ask what an agent costs to run and build: $0.99 to $2 a conversation or $20 to $30 a seat a month, and 200 to 1,400 build hours8,5,12.

How much does an AI agent cost?

Usage runs $0.99 to $2 per conversation on ready-made support agents, or $20 to $30 a person a month for AI seats8,10,9,5. Set-up and people's time add more. Our worked example of 1,000 invoices a month comes to about $53,000 to $65,000 in year one on a ready-made path, a Zavient estimate14,11.

How much does it cost to build an AI agent?

Published estimates put a custom agent at 200 to 1,400 hours of build work, plus 3 to 5 days per system it connects to12,11. Upkeep then runs 15% to 25% of the build each year18,19. Our worked example came to about $164,000 in year one, a Zavient estimate.

Is the AI model the biggest cost?

No, rarely: 10,000 documents a month cost about $35 on a small model, $690 on a mid model and $2,760 on a top model1,3. Training and checking the AI's work usually cost more15,17.

How much of the AI's work should people check?

Check more where a mistake costs more: 27% of firms review all gen-AI output, and a similar share check 20% or less16. Nearly 40% of AI time savings go to rework, so budget the checking17.

Should we buy a tool, turn on AI we already pay for, or build?

Most firms buy: 76% of AI use cases are bought, not built24. Bought or partner-built tools succeed about 67% of the time, internal builds about a third as often25. Build only when the work is core and no tool fits.

How long does it take to get an AI agent working?

Pilots on ready-made tools take 4 to 8 weeks20. Half of firms take 3 to 6 months from idea to production, and Gartner's average is about 29 weeks20.

Why do AI cost estimates go so wrong?

Gartner warns that leaders who don't know how AI costs scale can be off by 500% to 1,000%21. Usage grows with volume, and on some plans every token is billed on top of the seat fee27.

When should you pick something else?

Pick something else when fewer than 10 people do it, your data is scattered, the agent would chain many steps across systems, or someone proposes training your own model5,22,23,26.

  • Fewer than 10 people do this work: turn on AI seats you already pay for first5,6,7. Microsoft 365 Copilot, ChatGPT Business and Claude Team all qualify.
  • Your data is scattered or unknown: fix the data first. Gartner expects firms to abandon 60% of AI projects without AI-ready data22.
  • The agent would run a long chain of steps across systems: start with one step. Gartner expects over 40% of agentic AI projects to be canceled by 202723.
  • Someone proposes training your own model: that costs $5 million to $20 million up front. Off-the-shelf models with your documents cover most first projects26.
