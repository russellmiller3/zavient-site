What is generative AI consulting, and what does it change?

Generative AI consulting helps you pick a few writing, document and search tasks, choose safe tools and measure the result.1,2 What changes: your people draft and summarize faster, then check the output; in one trial, writing took 40% less time at higher quality.22,3 Redesigning the work makes or breaks it.4

What should you walk away with?

You should walk away with two or three ranked tasks, today's numbers, approved tools with a one-page rule, trained staff and a plan your team can run alone.1,3,8,10,12

  • You should walk away with a ranked list of two or three tasks, each with an owner and a metric. Most AI-using firms limit use to three or fewer tasks.1,6
  • Today's numbers, plus a test set of past work with known right answers. NIST says to check AI output against known right answers, not impressions.3,7
  • Approved business-tier tools and a one-page rule staff can follow. Most AI users bring their own tools to work, and two-thirds of mid-market firms set rules before pilots.8,9
  • Trained staff with a review habit. In BCG's survey of employees, five or more hours of training raised regular use from 67% to 79%.10,11
  • A plan your team can run without the advisor, with tool accounts and the test set in your firm's name. In RSM's 2025 survey of middle market decision-makers, 62% found generative AI harder to put in place than expected.12,13

Most firms of 40 to 500 staff need document help first. Get rules help before pilots and strategy help only when leaders cannot agree on a first task.1,8,6

Where does your team stay in charge?

Your team signs off every output that matters: the person who signs the work checks it, customer-facing answers go through the service lead, and experts keep judgement calls.14,16,17

  • Your team stays in charge of every sign-off: the person who signs the work checks it, like the controller for numbers. A US court fined lawyers $5,000 for filing AI-invented cases, citing their gatekeeping role.14,3
  • The customer service lead signs off anything AI says to customers. A Canadian tribunal held an airline responsible for its website chatbot's wrong answer.16,3
  • Experts keep the judgement calls AI handles badly. In a study of 758 consultants, AI users were 19 points less likely to be right on such a task.17,3
  • AI takes first drafts and search; your people keep the job and add oversight work. In Census data, 66% of AI-using firms only use AI to assist staff on tasks, and 2% report AI-related job decreases.1,5

What shapes can the work take?

Small teams start with one weekly writing task, mid-size firms with document-heavy work, and staff on personal AI tools move to an approved plan first.1,8

1. Small team, one writing task (about 40 to 100 staff)

Pick this when a team drafts the same kind of document every week. Writing is the leading generative AI task at US firms.1,22

  1. Ops leadPick one writing task and record today's time per item and error rate
  2. IT leadSwitch on one business-tier tool for a pilot group
  3. StaffTake five or more hours of hands-on training
  4. Team leadReview every output for the first month
  5. Ops leadCompare time and errors with the baseline, counting review time
  6. GM (general manager)Roll out to other teams or sites only when both numbers improve

Cost: Business seats list at $14 to $25 a user a month (vendor pages, read 26 Nov 2026).50,39 Training adds about $270 a person by our math: five hours at the BLS employer cost of $54.00 an hour.19,10

2. Mid-size firm, document-heavy work (about 100 to 500 staff)

Pick this when teams read contracts, claims, reports or files all day. Document analysis and information search lead generative AI use at US firms.1

  1. COOChoose two or three document tasks and name an owner for each
  2. AnalystBuild a test set of past documents with known right answers
  3. IT leadSet who may see which files before any tool connects
  4. Controller or reviewerSign off outputs before anyone acts on them
  5. Division headDecide go or no-go on a date set at the start

Cost: One seat at the $25 monthly list price costs $300 a year by our math.40,39 At the all-worker average of 2.08% of hours saved, by our math one full-time user returns about $2,340 a year (2,080 hours at $54.00).21,19

3. Shadow AI first (any size; staff already use unapproved personal AI tools)

Pick this when staff already paste work into personal AI accounts. Most AI users bring their own tools, and personal plans can train on prompts.8,37

  1. IT leadSurvey which AI tools staff use today
  2. Ops leadMove that work to a business-tier plan that does not train on prompts
  3. HR leadPublish a one-page usage rule and run training
  4. Team leadsSpot-check outputs every week

Cost: Microsoft's Copilot Business add-on lists at $21 a user a month billed yearly (read 26 Nov 2026).47 The UK cyber agency's shadow AI guidance is free.29

Why do most AI pilots stall?

Most AI pilots stall because they solve the wrong problem or track the wrong metric. In S&P Global's survey of IT and business professionals, the average firm scrapped 46% of trial projects.6,25

  • Most AI pilots stall because they solve the wrong problem. Misreading the problem or the metric was the top cause in RAND's study, so agree one measured task first.6,26
  • Pilots never reach daily use: in S&P Global's survey of IT and business professionals, the average firm scrapped 46% of trial projects. Set a go or no-go date and a production owner before the pilot starts.25,26
  • Confident wrong answers: courts have logged over 2,000 decisions on AI-invented content.15,3 A consulting report to a national government drew a partial refund over fake references, so a named reviewer checks every fact.28
  • Staff leak data through personal tools: in PagerDuty's survey, 34% had put customer data into public AI.30,29 Give them a business-tier plan and a short rule.38,49
  • AI used outside what it does well: consultants using AI on such a task were 19 points less likely to be right.17 Test each task separately and keep experts on judgement calls.18
  • Leaders keener than staff: in RSM's survey of middle market executives, 85% say leaders are more enthusiastic than employees.9 Only 36% of employees feel properly trained, so fund five or more hours of hands-on practice.10

How do you judge any AI advisor?

Judge any AI advisor by a test on your own past work: hidden known answers, pass criteria set before the demo, and results compared with today's baseline.31,3,32

  1. Judge any advisor by a test on your own past work, starting with today's numbers for one task: time per item, error rate and rework.31
  2. Pull 50 to 100 past items with known right answers, and keep them hidden from the advisor.3,33
  3. Write pass and fail criteria before any demo, not after.32,7
  4. Have the advisor run their approach on your items, in the tool you will actually use.31
  5. Score the output against the right answers, and have your reviewer check a sample. Only 27% of executives and managers in McKinsey's survey say all generative AI output is reviewed before use.3,4
  6. Compare time and errors with today's baseline, counting the time spent reviewing.31
  7. Price the lift: hours saved times employer cost, minus seats. BLS puts a full-time private-sector worker at $54.00 an hour worked (June 2026).19
  8. Ask where not to use AI. It helps on some tasks and hurts on others, so an advisor who says "everything" has not tested.17
  9. Re-test every month, because models and vendor terms change.7,43

Questions buyers ask about Generative AI consulting

What does a generative AI consultant do?

A generative AI consultant helps you pick a few writing, document and search tasks, set safe tools and rules, train staff and measure results.1,4 Good ones redesign the work around review, since the AI can be confidently wrong.3

How much does it cost to hire an AI consultant?

US federal contracts cap the hourly rate for AI specialists at a median of about $194, a ceiling rather than a price paid.51 Most consultants quote a fixed project fee or an hourly rate, so the scope sets the total, while Small Business Development Centers advise for free.52,53,34 Business-tier AI seats add $14 to $25 a user a month.50,39

Is generative AI consulting worth it for a mid-sized firm?

Yes, but only if you leave with a measured result on your own work.6,3 In RSM's 2025 survey of middle market decision-makers, 70% said their firms need outside help.12 MIT's directional study found partnerships reached use about twice as often as internal builds.27

Will generative AI take my staff's jobs?

No, not on current evidence: 66% of AI-using US firms only use AI to assist staff, and 2% report AI-related job decreases.1 In a study of 5,172 support agents, the least experienced gained most, resolving 30% more issues an hour.18

Is it safe to let staff use ChatGPT with company data?

Yes, on a business-tier plan, but not on a personal one. Personal ChatGPT may train on chats unless the user opts out; ChatGPT Business does not by default.37,38 Our table above compares four vendors.

Should we build a custom AI tool or use what we already have?

Use what you already have first, and build only when a test on your own work shows a gap.27,3 Our custom AI solutions guide covers when building pays.

When should you not hire help?

Don't hire help when your team just needs a tool and a rule, general business advice, or hands-on training, or when the task should stay with your experts.39,34,10,17

  • Don't hire help when your team just needs a tool and a rule. Buy ChatGPT Business, Claude Team, Microsoft 365 Copilot Business or Gemini in Google Workspace, and use the vendor's admin setup.39,44
  • You need general business advice, not AI work: a local Small Business Development Center gives free one-on-one advising.34,35
  • Your staff need skills, not a project: run five or more hours of hands-on practice in the tool you already pay for. BCG's survey ties that much training to more regular use.10
  • The task is high stakes and outside what AI does well: keep it with your experts.17,14
