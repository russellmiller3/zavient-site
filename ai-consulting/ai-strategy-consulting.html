What is AI strategy consulting, and what does it change?

AI strategy consulting is outside help to decide where AI pays first, in what order and under what rules, before anyone builds.3,15 What changes: your team redesigns one ranked workflow first, with a baseline number to beat and named owners.15,14,2

What should you walk away with?

AI strategy help should leave you with a ranked list of workflows, a baseline number, a 90-day plan, an AI use policy and a list of what not to do.19,10,15,2

  • One ranked list of workflows, scored on value, data readiness, risk and owner, with the top one named.19 Firms with fewer failed AI projects weigh risk, compliance and data when they pick.27
  • A baseline number for that workflow, measured before any tool is bought: hours, cycle time, error rate or cost.10,3 High performers are twice as likely to have set ways to measure AI's impact.14
  • A 90-day plan with an owner for each step and a go-or-stop date.15 Leaders need "the courage to exit experiments that are not delivering".22
  • A governance baseline: an AI use policy, a list of the AI already in use, and who signs off.2 Of staff who use AI, 78% bring their own tools to work.28
  • A written list of what not to do, and why each idea was cut.10 Chasing new technology instead of the problem is a common root cause of AI project failure.11

Where does your team stay in charge?

Your team keeps the calls: the CEO or COO owns AI risk, process owners pick the workflow and check output, and people decide anything about individuals.2,5 A named person also signs off anything AI tells customers.7

  • CEO or COO: owns decisions about AI risk.2 Firms whose CEO oversees AI governance report more bottom-line impact from AI.15
  • Process owners (ops lead, controller, underwriter): pick the workflow and check AI output.2,1 AI helps them work: support agents with an AI assistant resolved 15% more issues an hour.12
  • A named person signs off anything AI tells customers. A tribunal held Air Canada liable for its chatbot's wrong fare advice, and rejected the claim the bot was separate.7
  • People keep decisions about individuals, such as hiring, lending, insurance, housing and health care.5 Colorado's SB26-189 covers AI used to make, guide or assist those decisions, from 1 Jan 2027.5,6

What shapes can the work take?

AI strategy work takes three shapes: one workflow first for owner-led firms, a ranked portfolio with rules for mid-size firms, or rules first where AI touches decisions about people.18,20,5

1. Owner-led firm, about 40 to 120 staff: one workflow first

The CEO still owns AI, there are many ideas and no data team. Winners "pick one pain point, execute well, and partner smartly".18,17

  1. CEOName the three jobs that cost the most time or errors
  2. Ops leadTime one of them for two weeks to get a baseline
  3. AdvisorScore the three on value, data, risk and owner
  4. CEOPick one and set a go-or-stop date
  5. Team leadRun a 30-day pilot, with a person checking every AI output
  6. CEOGo, change or stop on the date

Cost: Free first pass: an SBDC advisor, or a subsidized Manufacturing Extension Partnership (MEP) center if you make things.9,8 For paid help, ask for a fixed price and a fixed scope.

2. Mid-size firm, about 120 to 500 staff: a ranked portfolio plus rules

Several division heads want AI, staff already use their own tools, and a board or lender wants a plan.28 Of mid-market AI users, 79% have a roadmap but only 37% call it well formed.20

  1. COOSponsor it; ask each division head for their two costliest jobs
  2. IT or ops leadList the AI tools already in use
  3. AdvisorScore the candidates and draft the AI use policy
  4. Division headsConfirm a baseline for each candidate
  5. COORank the top 2 or 3 and name an owner for each
  6. CEOSign off the 90-day plan and the policy
  7. OwnersPilot at one site or team first, with a monthly review

Cost: Ask for a fixed price and scope, with any build quoted separately. Zavient's own sprint, for example, runs a fixed 30 days.30

3. Firm where AI touches decisions about people: rules first

You lend, insure, hire or run health care, and AI already touches those decisions.5 Most executives lack strong confidence they'd pass an AI governance audit within 90 days.22

  1. Compliance leadList every AI system that touches a decision about a person
  2. AdvisorMap each one to the AI framework of NIST, the US standards body, and to state rules
  3. Division head (e.g. head of lending)Decide where a person must review
  4. COOApprove the policy and how often it is reviewed
  5. ThenRun recipe 1 for the first value workflow

Cost: The NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) and its Playbook are free.1,2 The international AI management standard, ISO/IEC 42001, costs CHF 225 to buy; certification is extra.4

Why do most AI pilots stall?

Most AI pilots stall because of people and process, not models: the wrong problem, data that isn't ready, no baseline and no end date.16,11,21,27

  • Wrong problem or wrong metric is RAND's most common root cause of failure.11 Fix: write down the business metric and its owner before picking a tool.
  • Data not ready: it is mid-market firms' top AI barrier, and Gartner expects most projects without AI-ready data to be dropped.21,25 Fix: check the data on a real sample in week one.
  • Chasing the tech, not the problem: Gartner expects over 40% of agentic AI projects to be canceled by end of 2027.26,11 Fix: first ask whether a rule, template or existing tool does the job.10
  • Skipping people and process, where about 70% of AI challenges sit.16,21 Fix: redesign the workflow with the team that does it, the biggest driver of profit from AI.15
  • No baseline, so no proof: about a third of firms don't track AI value yet.36,23 Fix: measure today's numbers before the pilot starts.
  • Trials with no end date: the average firm scrapped 46% of its AI proofs of concept (trial projects) before production.27,24 Fix: set a go-or-stop date, and stop what misses it.22

How do you judge any AI advisor?

Judge any AI advisor by testing their advice on 50 to 100 real past cases they never saw, against a two-week baseline and the cheapest option.35,10 Also ask for their scoring rubric, and agree a stop date before the pilot.27,22

  1. Record today's numbers for one workflow for two weeks: volume, hours, errors and cycle time.10,3
  2. Ask to see the scoring rubric, plus the scores of the ideas they cut.27
  3. Hold back 50 to 100 real past cases the advisor never sees, and try the advice on them. Benchmarks should mirror the real task, edge cases included.35,37
  4. Compare it with the cheapest option: a rule, a template or an off-the-shelf tool. Bought or partnered tools reached deployment about twice as often as internal builds.10,17
  5. Try a hard case on purpose. On a task outside AI's reach, consultants using AI were 19 points less likely to be right.13
  6. Price the lift: hours saved times loaded hourly cost, minus the cost to run it. US private employers paid $46.89 per hour worked in June 2026; use your own pay data first.34
  7. Agree a stop rule and a date before the pilot starts.22 RAND advises picking problems you'd commit a team to for at least a year.11
  8. Check what they told you not to do, and why.10 Red flag: a deck with no named workflow, owner or stop date.
  9. Get the rubric, scores, baseline and policy as files you keep, so the work outlives the engagement.

Questions buyers ask about AI strategy consulting

What's the difference between AI strategy and AI automation consulting?

Strategy decides what gets built, in what order and why; automation builds it and runs it. Our AI automation consulting guide covers the build. Either way, define the business value first, as NIST's AI framework asks.3

How much does AI strategy consulting cost?

A first pass can cost $0 through a free SBDC advisor, but we found no neutral survey of paid AI strategy fees to quote.9 Ask for a fixed price and a fixed scope, with any build quoted separately. If you make things, MEP centers are subsidized.8

Is AI consulting actually worth it for small and mid-sized businesses?

Only for a problem with a number attached: winners pick one pain point.18 Of middle-market firms, 70% say they need outside help to get the most from AI.19 A free SBDC advisor is a fair first step.9

How long does an AI strategy take?

Weeks, not months, for a focused version. Zavient's fixed-scope sprint runs 30 days.30 Mid-market top performers went from pilot to full use in about 90 days, against nine months or more at large firms.17

What is an AI readiness assessment?

An AI readiness assessment is a check of your strategy, data, governance, skills and infrastructure. Free vendor self-checks exist: Microsoft's takes about 45 minutes, and Cisco's covers six areas.33,38 Gartner found 63% of data leaders lack or are unsure of AI-ready data practices.25

Who should own AI strategy at a mid-size company?

A senior operator with budget, often the CEO or COO.2,15 Few firms hire a dedicated AI leader: in our count, builder job ads outnumber AI-owner ads about 56 to 1.31

When should you not hire help?

Skip strategy help when you already know the workflow, data and owner, when budget is tight, when you only need AI rules, or when staff just need training.17,9,2,28

  • You already know the workflow, the data and the owner: skip strategy, and buy a tool or scope a build with automation help. Bought or partnered tools reached deployment about 67% of the time.17
  • Under about 50 staff or on a tight budget: start with a free SBDC advisor, or an MEP center if you make things.9,8 New York's MEP guide recommends a pilot-first approach.39
  • You only need a governance baseline: use the free NIST AI RMF Playbook.2 Add ISO/IEC 42001 if customers ask for a certificate.4
  • Staff just need to use AI well day to day: an approved tool plus training, not a strategy project.28 Most small-business owners (73%) say more training would help them put AI to work.29
