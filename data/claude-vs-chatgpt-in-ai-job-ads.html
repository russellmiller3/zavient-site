Claude has caught OpenAI in US AI job ads

Claude and OpenAI each appear in 268 of 2,046 US AI job ads (21 to 25 Nov 2026)1. Slightly more employers name Claude: 206 against 1921.

By Zavient · updated 2026-11-25

What we found

  • Claude (Anthropic) and OpenAI's GPT models each appear in 268 of the 2,046 ads1.
  • Counted by employer, 206 employers name Claude and 192 name OpenAI1.
  • Google's Gemini trails far behind, at 53 employers1.
  • Open models are rare: Meta's Llama at 23 employers, Mistral at 13 and DeepSeek at 31.
  • Much of Claude's showing comes from coding. Ads often ask for Claude Code alongside Cursor or GitHub Copilot1.
  • Spending data points the same way. In October 2026, 44.8% of US businesses on Ramp paid for Anthropic and 40.6% for OpenAI2.
  • Menlo Ventures put Anthropic at 40% of enterprise LLM spend in 2025, against OpenAI's 27%3.

How we counted

  • We pulled the 3,000 newest US job ads with AI or machine-learning job titles, from the JobsPipe job index1.
  • We removed reposts (the same title from the same company), leaving 2,046 ads from 1,172 employers.
  • A model counts when the ad's description names it as a whole word, for example 'Claude', 'Anthropic', 'OpenAI', 'ChatGPT' or 'GPT-4'.
  • Each maker's own job ads are left out of its count, because every Anthropic ad names Anthropic. We hand-checked samples of the matches.
  • All 3,000 ads were posted from 21 to 25 Nov 2026, so this is a five-day snapshot. We will repeat it monthly.
