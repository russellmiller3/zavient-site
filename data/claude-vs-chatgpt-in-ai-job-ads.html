Claude has caught ChatGPT in US AI job ads

In 2,046 US AI job ads posted between 21 and 25 Nov 2026, Claude and OpenAI's models appear in the same number of ads, and slightly more employers name Claude.

What I found

Claude (Anthropic) and OpenAI's GPT models each appear in 268 of the 2,046 ads. Counted by employer, 206 employers name Claude and 192 name OpenAI.

Google's Gemini trails far behind, at 53 employers. Open models are rare: Meta's Llama at 23 employers, Mistral at 13 and DeepSeek at 3.

Much of Claude's showing comes from coding: ads often ask for experience with Claude Code alongside Cursor or GitHub Copilot.

The job ads match what spending data shows. Ramp's AI Index put Anthropic ahead of OpenAI in US business adoption in October 2026, at 44.8% to 40.6%.

How I counted

  • We pulled the 3,000 newest US job ads with AI or machine-learning job titles posted between 21 and 25 Nov 2026, from the jobspipe job index.
  • We removed reposts (the same title from the same company), leaving 2,046 ads from 1,172 employers.
  • A model counts when the ad's description names it as a whole word, for example 'Claude', 'Anthropic', 'OpenAI', 'ChatGPT' or 'GPT-4'.
  • Each maker's own job ads are left out of its count, because every Anthropic ad names Anthropic. We hand-checked samples of the matches.
  • The 3,000 newest ads were all posted from 21 to 25 Nov 2026, so this is a five-day snapshot, not a month. A monthly pull now tracks how these counts change.
