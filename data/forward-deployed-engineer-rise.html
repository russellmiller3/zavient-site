Forward deployed engineer is as common as machine learning engineer in AI job ads

Forward deployed engineers build AI on site with the customer. In US AI job ads, the title is as common as machine learning engineer: 304 ads against 3061.

What we found

  • Forward deployed engineers build software alongside a customer, on the customer's own data.
  • In 2,046 recent US AI job ads, 304 carry that title, from 213 employers1.
  • That is level with machine learning engineer, at 306 ads from 210 employers1.
  • Only data scientist (479 ads) and AI engineer (435 ads) are more common1.
  • The title follows Palantir's model of sending engineers to the customer. Palantir pitches a five-day bootcamp: 'From 0 to use case in 5 days'2.
  • Large consulting firms hire for the role too1.

How we counted

  • We pulled the 3,000 newest US job ads with AI or machine-learning job titles, from the JobsPipe job index1.
  • We removed reposts (same title, same company), leaving 2,046 ads.
  • Each ad goes to the first title group its job title matches, in a fixed order, so no ad is counted twice.
  • Titles that match none of the groups are left out of the chart.
  • All 3,000 ads were posted from 21 to 25 Nov 2026, so this is a five-day snapshot, not a trend. We will repeat it monthly.
