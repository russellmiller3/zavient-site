What I found

Forward deployed engineers build software alongside a customer, on the customer's own data. In 2,046 recent US AI job ads, 304 carry that title, from 213 employers.

That is level with machine learning engineer, at 306 ads from 210 employers. Only data scientist (479) and AI engineer (435) are more common.

The title follows Palantir's model of sending engineers to the customer. Palantir's AIP Bootcamp promises 'from 0 to use case in 5 days' on the customer's own data.

Consulting firms hire for it too: Deloitte, for example, advertises a Senior Anthropic forward deployed engineer role.

How I counted

  • We pulled the 3,000 newest US job ads with AI or machine-learning job titles posted between 21 and 25 Nov 2026, from the jobspipe job index, and removed reposts (same title, same company).
  • Each ad goes to the first title group its job title matches, in a fixed order, so no ad is counted twice.
  • Titles that match none of the groups are left out of the chart.
  • The job index keeps about four months of ads, so this is a snapshot, not a trend; we will repeat it monthly.
