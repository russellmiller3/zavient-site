What AI jobs pay in the US, by job title

We took the bottom of each ad's pay range, then the median across ads. Heads of AI post the highest, at $220,6001. Data scientists post the lowest, at $130,0001.

By Zavient · updated 2026-11-25

What this means for you: An ML engineer, an AI engineer and a data scientist post about $456,000 a year in base pay, before benefits1. Compare that with buying or bringing in help. Buy, build or hire: what each costs →

What we found

  • Research and leadership roles pay most1.
  • Head or director of AI: a median $220,600. AI research scientist: about $197,000. Applied scientist: $184,5001.
  • Machine learning engineers post a median $170,6001.
  • That is ahead of forward deployed engineers ($161,359) and AI engineers ($155,300)1.
  • Data scientists post the lowest median, $130,000: about $40,000 below machine learning engineers1.
  • These are the bottoms of posted pay ranges, so real offers often run higher.

How we counted

  • From 2,046 recent US AI job ads (reposts removed), we kept the 1,038 that state a yearly minimum between $40,000 and $2 million1.
  • The figure is the median of each ad's posted minimum, the bottom of the stated range.
  • Groups with fewer than 30 ads are marked 'small sample'. Treat them as less certain.
  • Pay is as posted. Some states require pay ranges in ads, so ads from those states are over-represented.
  • All ads were posted from 21 to 25 Nov 2026, so this is a five-day snapshot.
