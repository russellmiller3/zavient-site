What AI jobs pay in the US, by job title

What I found

Research and leadership roles pay most: head or director of AI at a median $220,600, AI research scientist at $196,965, applied scientist at $184,500.

Machine learning engineers post a median $170,600, ahead of forward deployed engineers ($161,359) and AI engineers ($155,300).

Data scientists post the lowest median, $130,000, about $40,000 below machine learning engineers.

These are the minimums of posted pay ranges, so actual offers often run higher.

How I counted

  • From 2,046 recent US AI job ads (reposts removed), we kept the 1,038 that state a yearly minimum between $40,000 and $2 million.
  • The figure is the median of each ad's posted minimum, the bottom of the stated range.
  • Groups with fewer than 30 ads (head of AI, applied scientist, AI product manager) are less certain; their ad counts are shown.
  • Pay is as posted; some states require pay ranges in ads, so ads from those states are over-represented.
