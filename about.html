Why Zavient exists

Mid-size companies hear a lot about AI and get little help choosing where to start. We are a small firm, and we sell no software.

We sit in between. We tell you:

  • Which workflow to start with, in any part of the business.
  • Whether to buy or build, and what to test before you spend.
  • How AI can take the reading, matching and first drafts, so your team keeps the judgment.

Most firms already use AI this way. 66% of firms that use AI use it only to help with tasks1.

How we work

  1. Free 30-minute diagnostic. Bring one workflow and what it costs you today. You leave knowing buy or build, what to test, the rules and rough cost, with no obligation.
  2. 30-day AI Sprint, fixed scope. You get:
    • The 2 or 3 AI bets that pay back first, ranked by payoff and effort.
    • Specs your team or a vendor can build from.
    • A written case for your board, lender or partners.
    • A 90-day plan with owners, and what not to do.
  3. Ongoing advisory, if you want it. Two working sessions a month, plus questions answered between them, while the work ships. Quarterly commitment, renewed only if both sides want to.

Building the software itself is quoted per job, or done by your team or a vendor from our specs. You own the specs, the code and the results.

How we check our numbers

  • Every market claim has a source. Each number links to a numbered source at the foot of the page, with two or more sources where they exist.
  • Our own tests show their method. In FinExtractBench, our test of AI on financial documents, we wrote the right answers first, before any tool ran2. Each page lists the sample, what was measured and the limits.
  • We say what the numbers don't prove. A test on 30 documents is not a test on your documents, so we tell you to re-run it on your own files.
  • We use AI to help research and draft. Every number still links to the place it came from, so you can check it yourself.
  • Corrections. If you find an error, email hello@zavient.com. We fix it and update the page's date.

What we won't do

  • Plan AI whose main goal is cutting your staff.
  • Recommend tools your team can't check.
  • Lock you in. You keep the specs and can build with anyone.
  • Hand you a 200-page slide deck.
  • Run AI experiments with no measure of what they're worth. If we can't find the return, we'll tell you.
