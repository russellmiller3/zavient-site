Zavient AI-work site: data flow and sample pagesHow the Zavient AI-work site turns data into leads

Where the data comes from, how it becomes pages, and how a visitor becomes a lead. Press Play, or step through it.

A · Fill the database

  1. A1 Pull public scores and map each to a job.
  2. A2 Pull every model with its price and hosts.
  3. A3 Catch news: a new model or a company AI rollout.
  4. A4 Run our own benchmark: 30 finance questions to 20 models.
  5. A5 Save scores: right answers and cost per correct answer.
  6. A6 Build the pages from the database.

B · A visitor becomes a lead

  1. B1 A buyer searches Google for 'insurance underwriting ai' and lands on our page.
  2. B2 The page shows best models for the job, cost per task, tools and our benchmark.
  3. B3 The buyer fills in the offer form at the end of the page.
  4. B4 The lead lands with Russell, with what the buyer read.
  5. B5 Paid benchmark: the buyer's sample documents go to the runner.
  6. B6 The runner tests 20 models on the buyer's documents.
  7. B7 The report shows which model wins and what it costs.

C · A practitioner adds a report

  1. C1 A practitioner reports what worked for them.
  2. C2 Check it, then add it to the job's page as a practitioner report.
