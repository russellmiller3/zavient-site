Which AI pages are worth writing

Google search demand for three needs a consulting site could serve: moving off paid AI models, testing models, and building your own AI test. US searches, checked 25 Nov 2026.

Write three pages about running AI on your own computers: “on premise llm”, “self host llm” and “openai api alternatives”. Together they get about 8,900 Google searches a month, and small sites hold Google's first page today. Skip pages about building your own AI test: only about 80 searches a month.
