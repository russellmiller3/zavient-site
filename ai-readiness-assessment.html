Is your company ready for AI?

For companies of 40 to 500 people. 3 minutes, no sign-up. Find your first AI job and the one gap to fix first.

How the score works
  • Nine scored questions across five areas: the work, the data, the people, the guardrails and the systems.
  • Questions about your company aren't scored; they tailor your result.
  • Each scored answer is worth 0 to 3 points. 27 points is a perfect score, shown out of 100. Each area gets its own 0 to 100 bar.
  • Three stages: 0 to 39, Clear the runway. 40 to 69, Ready for a first win. 70 to 100, Ready to scale.
  • The areas draw on three published readiness frameworks: Cisco's, Microsoft's and the US standards body NIST's.[^1][^3][^6] We cut them down for a company without a data team.
  • Your answers stay in your browser; we don't see them.
