      How the math works
      1. Items AI handles a month = volume × AI share × share routed through AI.
      2. Minutes moved per item = minutes today − review minutes − (redo rate × minutes today).
      3. Money per month = hours moved × loaded cost × 56%. Freed minutes scattered across a day do not all become output. The 56% is Gartner's worked example: a 20% leak, then a 70% harvesting ratio (Gartner panel, 2024).
      4. Loaded cost per hour = BLS mean hourly pay (OEWS May 2025) × 1 + (benefits − paid leave) ÷ (wages + paid leave), from ECEC June 2026: 1.28 to 1.31. Pay already includes paid leave, so we leave it out of the benefits. Where no single job fits, we use ECEC total pay and benefits per hour.
      5. Value ramps up in a straight line over the first months, while run cost starts in month 1. New technology tends to dip before it pays (US Census Bureau, 2025).
      6. Payback month = the first month the running total, minus the setup cost, reaches zero. Not discounted.

      A planning estimate, not a forecast. Self-reported time savings often run high, so time a sample of real items (METR, 2025).
