Your team stays in charge

Our focus is AI that helps people do more, not AI that replaces them. Most firms already use it that way. In a Census survey of US firms, 66% of AI users used it only to help with tasks, and just 2% of firms cut jobs because of AI2.

  • People keep the decision. AI reads, matches and drafts. A person approves the loan, the claim or the payment.
  • Every answer can be checked. We pick tools that show where each answer came from, so your team can check it.

How we check our numbers

We test AI tools on real documents before we recommend them. For our own benchmark, we wrote the right answers to 30 fund reports first, before any tool ran. The two best AI models made zero mistakes3.

We also count the whole cost, not just the software. The median company spends $6.00 to process one invoice, all in4.

But 30 reports is a small test. Even with zero misses, the real miss rate could be as high as 1 in 105. That is why we test on your own files before you buy. How we source and check every page is on our About page.
