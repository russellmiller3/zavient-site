Your team stays in charge

Our focus is AI that helps people do more, not AI that replaces them. Most firms already use it that way.

  • Most AI use is help, not replacement. 66% of firms that use AI use it only to help with tasks1.
  • Few firms cut jobs over AI. Just 2% did1.
  • People keep the decision. AI reads, matches and drafts. A person approves the loan, the claim or the payment.
  • Every answer can be checked. We pick tools that show where each answer came from.

How we check our numbers

  • We write the right answers first, then test. On 30 fund reports, the two best AI models made zero mistakes2.
  • We count the whole cost. The median company spends $6.00 to process one invoice, all in3.
  • 30 reports is a small test. The true miss rate could still be up to 1 in 104. So we re-test on your files before you buy.
