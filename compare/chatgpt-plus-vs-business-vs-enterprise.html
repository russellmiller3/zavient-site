ChatGPT Plus vs Business vs Enterprise: which should your staff get?

Bottom line
  • Pick Business Standard, billed yearly, for most firms under 200 staff: same $20 a seat as Plus, with admin controls2,3.
  • Pick Plus only for one person's own, non-confidential work; OpenAI may train on those chats unless they opt out1,12.
  • Pick Enterprise if you need automatic offboarding, audit logs, a BAA (health-data contract) or over 200 seats2,5.
  • Pick a monthly Business pilot first if use is unproven; firms leave 36% of software licences unused2,32.
  • Pick neither until you check the AI chat already in your Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace plan36,37.

What is the real difference?

Plus, Business and Enterprise run near-identical ChatGPT, but the plan decides who controls accounts and data1,2. Plus belongs to one person, and OpenAI may train on those chats unless they opt out12. Business gives the firm sign-in, admin and no training by default; Enterprise adds audit logs and retention control2,11.

What does each ChatGPT plan cost, and who controls it?

Plus is $20 a month on a personal account. Business Standard is $20-25 a seat with company admin; Enterprise has no public price and adds audit-log feeds.3,12,2,5,10 Business Premium seats cost $100-125 a month, and third parties report Enterprise at about $60 a seat.2,5,23

Which one fits your team?

Business Standard, billed yearly, fits most firms under 200 staff. Plus suits one person, and Enterprise suits firms needing audit logs, a BAA or over 200 seats.2,3,5

1. Pilot in one unit: 8-15 staff for 60-90 days

You want proof in one unit or site before a firm-wide spend. Use Business billed monthly, not Plus, because pilot staff will still paste company data2,12.

  1. Ops leadPick two or three repeat tasks and 8-12 volunteers from two departments
  2. ITCreate the Business workspace, verify the email domain, turn on company sign-in (SSO) and two-step sign-in (MFA)
  3. ITChoose which connected apps are switched on
  4. Department headName who checks AI output before it leaves the team
  5. Ops leadTime each task before the pilot, so there is a before-and-after number
  6. Ops leadRead weekly use in the admin analytics dashboard
  7. Ops lead and financeAt day 60-90, move to yearly billing or stop

Cost: 10 seats × $25 × 3 months = $750; 12 seats = $900, at list prices read 26 Nov 20262,5. OpenAI's pages differ on the minimum seat count, so check at checkout5,8.

2. Whole-firm rollout, 40-200 staff

Your firm has no BAA or data-residency rule and stays under Business's 200-seat cap5,6. Buy Standard yearly for everyone who will use it, and Premium only for proven heavy users2,5.

  1. Ops leadWrite a one-page AI use policy: what data may go in, who reviews what
  2. ITSet up SSO and a verified domain, and make auto-join need approval
  3. ITKeep a manual offboarding checklist, since Business has no SCIM
  4. ITHelp staff with personal Plus accounts move or separate their history
  5. Department headsPick uses for each role and name a reviewer for each
  6. FinanceSet credit usage alerts if you buy extra credits
  7. Ops leadReview seats each quarter and reassign idle ones

Cost: Worked example, 120 staff billed yearly: 108 Standard and 12 Premium seats come to $40,320 a year2,5. All Standard, the same 120 seats cost $28,800 a year2.

3. Regulated firm, or more than 200 staff

Pick this past 200 seats, because new Business workspaces stop there6. Also pick it if you need a BAA, retention control, audit logs, data residency or automatic offboarding2,5.

  1. Compliance or legalList hard needs: BAA, retention period, residency, eDiscovery
  2. Ops leadGet a written quote with seat minimum, included credits and true-up terms (charges for seats added mid-contract)
  3. ITConnect SCIM to your identity system and set role-based access
  4. IT and securityCopy Compliance API logs to your archive; OpenAI keeps them 30 days
  5. ITPlan the personal-account move; on Enterprise, staff get 7 days to choose
  6. Department headsKeep sign-off rules for each role

Cost: OpenAI publishes no Enterprise price2. Third parties report about $60 a seat a month with a 150-seat minimum, about $108,000 a year at the floor23,22.

Where does your team stay in charge?

Your IT team controls access and which systems ChatGPT can read, and a named reviewer checks AI output before it leaves the firm.8,18,24

  • IT decides who gets in and who gets out. On Business, IT removes leavers by hand because there is no SCIM; Enterprise automates it8,9,10.
  • The ops lead and IT decide which company systems ChatGPT can read. Business admins choose which apps are on; on Enterprise, all apps start switched off18.
  • A named reviewer in each team, such as the controller for finance drafts, checks AI output before it leaves the firm. NIST advises checking sources and citations, and ChatGPT Work can pause for a person to approve key steps24,15.
  • Licensed staff keep their professional duties, and compliance owns the record. Lawyers keep confidentiality duties, and FINRA says its supervision rules apply to AI25,26,19.

What does each look like in practice?

ChatGPT itself works almost the same on all three plans. What changes is who controls the account, the data and the audit trail.1,2

Goes inComes out
Illustrative: a 90-person distributor where six staff expense personal Plus accounts. A buyer pastes a supplier's price list into a personal chat to draft a reorder email. The firm can't see or delete that chat, and training is on unless the buyer opted out12,13.After: those six and 30 more get Business Standard seats at the same $20 a month, billed yearly2. What do teams get wrong when they choose?

Teams most often leave staff on personal accounts, pay for seats nobody uses, and keep risky workspace defaults on.27,32,8

  • Letting staff keep personal accounts: 32.3% of workplace ChatGPT use runs through them, and 57% of surveyed workers admit entering sensitive data27,28. Fix: buy a workspace and move people over with OpenAI's onboarding steps20.
  • Paying for seats nobody uses: firms leave 36% of software licences unused, and only 30% of one UK government Copilot pilot used it daily32,35. Fix: start monthly with volunteers, check admin analytics and reassign idle seats2,5.
  • Leaving workspace defaults on: Business members can invite others, and each accepted invite is a paid seat8,7. Fix: an owner reviews pending invites, auto-join needs approval, and IT runs an offboarding checklist8,9.
  • Assuming Business covers regulated data: self-serve Business offers no BAAs, Zero Data Retention or invoicing, and OpenAI points you to a contracted plan5. Fix: if you need a BAA, residency or eDiscovery, price Enterprise from day one2.
  • Planning a Business rollout past 200 people: since 24 Aug 2026, new Business workspaces cap at 200 paid seats, and support can't raise it6,8. Fix: past about 150 staff, get an Enterprise quote before the workspace fills2.
  • Budgeting seats, not usage: from December, Plus and Business share one allowance across ChatGPT and Codex, and longer Work tasks use more16,15. Fix: set credit usage alerts, since 78% of IT leaders report surprise AI charges17,33.

Questions buyers ask before they choose

Is ChatGPT Business worth it over Plus for a team?

Yes, on yearly billing: a Standard seat costs $20 a month, the same as Plus2,3. It adds company sign-in, admin controls and no training on your data by default2,12. Billed monthly, it costs $252.

Can admins see employees' ChatGPT Business chats?

Yes, plan as if they can. OpenAI's privacy page says Business admins can view, export and delete members' conversations11. On Enterprise, compliance teams pull logs through the Compliance API19.

Can we buy ChatGPT Business for just a few people?

Yes, but OpenAI's pages differ on the minimum5,8. One help article says one paid seat starts a workspace; the pricing page says from two users5,1. Check at checkout.

Is ChatGPT Team the same as ChatGPT Business?

Yes: OpenAI renamed Team to Business on 29 Aug 2025, and existing contracts stayed in effect6.

What is the difference between Chat and Work in ChatGPT?

Chat answers questions and drafts short pieces, while Work is an agent for multi-step tasks that end in a file you review14,15. Work runs on Plus, Business and Enterprise, and shares usage limits with Codex39,21.

How much does ChatGPT Enterprise cost?

About $60 a seat a month with a 150-seat minimum, by third-party reports; OpenAI publishes no price and quotes each deal23,22,2. Treat that as a planning number and get a written quote.

Should you buy a tool or build your own?

Buy: Buy a ChatGPT plan when the job is everyday writing, research and analysis for many staff30. Most enterprise AI is now bought: 76% of solutions in 2025, up from 53% in 202430. MIT found bought or partnered tools reached deployment about twice as often as internal builds29,40.

Build: Build when your own data or rules are the edge, such as one narrow workflow tied to your systems29. A build runs on OpenAI's API (developer access), billed apart from ChatGPT; Business includes up to $500 a month in API credits, per OpenAI5,2. Gartner says at least half of GenAI projects were dropped after proof of concept, so start small31.

When should you pick neither?

Pick neither if your Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace plan already includes AI chat, or you need one narrow workflow rather than chat for everyone.36,37,29

  • You already pay for Microsoft 365: Copilot Chat comes at no extra cost on eligible plans36. Microsoft 365 Copilot Business adds answers drawn from your email and files for $21 a user a month on top, billed yearly36.
  • You run Google Workspace: Business Standard, at $14 a user a month billed yearly, includes Gemini in Gmail, Docs and Meet37,38. Google says it doesn't train models on your company data37.
  • You need one narrow workflow, not a chat tool for everyone: a purpose-built tool or a small API build fits better29. MIT found generic chat tools stall in critical workflows that need memory and customisation29.
  • Staff won't use it yet: fix the task list and training first35. In one UK government pilot of 1,000 Copilot licences, only 30% used it daily34,35.
