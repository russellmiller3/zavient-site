ChatGPT vs Claude vs Copilot: which should your team roll out?

Bottom line
  • Pick Copilot if your mail, files and meetings live in Microsoft 365 and you want audit logs there.29,34

Which one fits your team?

Copilot fits a Microsoft 365 firm, ChatGPT or Claude a Google or mixed stack, and a regulated firm or one past 150 seats weighs enterprise tiers.29,17,3,5

1. Microsoft 365 firm, 40-150 staff

Your mail, files and Teams meetings already live in Microsoft 365.29 Start with Copilot Chat for everyone, then buy paid seats for proven heavy users.31,29

  1. IT adminTurn on Copilot Chat for all staff at no extra cost
  2. IT adminRun Microsoft's readiness check and fix overshared SharePoint sites
  3. COOWrite a one-page use policy: what data goes in, who reviews what
  4. Ops leadPick 10-30 heavy email and meeting users in one unit or site for paid Copilot Business
  5. IT adminSet a spending limit for per-use agents such as Cowork
  6. Department headsName a reviewer for anything that leaves the firm
  7. Ops leadAt day 60-90, add seats or reassign idle ones

Cost: By our math, 25 paid seats × $21 × 12 = $6,300 a year at list, read 26 Nov 2026.29 Bought by 31 Dec 2026, the $18 promo makes year one $5,400 by our math.29

2. Google Workspace or mixed-stack firm, 40-150 staff

Your mail and files sit in Gmail and Drive, or across several tools.14,59 ChatGPT Business and Claude Team cost the same $20 a seat billed yearly, so pilot one against the other.17,3

  1. IT or ownerCreate the workspace, verify the domain, turn on company sign-in (SSO)
  2. ITSwitch on only Drive, Gmail and Calendar, and keep approval before send
  3. Ops leadTime three repeat tasks first, so there is a before-and-after number
  4. Ops leadRun a 60-90 day pilot with 10-20 people in one unit or site, on monthly billing

3. Regulated firm, or 150-500 staff

Claude Team stops at 150 seats, new ChatGPT Business workspaces at 200 and Copilot Business at 300.3,18,29 Go to an enterprise tier if you need audit logs, automatic offboarding (SCIM) or retention control.5,17

  1. Compliance leadList hard needs: health-data contract (BAA), retention period, legal hold

Where does your team stay in charge?

The COO owns the rollout, IT decides what the assistant can read, and a person checks the output and presses send.12,13,51

  • The COO sets the usage policy and owns the rollout; IT decides what the assistant can read. Claude and ChatGPT admins switch on each connector, and Copilot sees only what each person can already open.12,22,31
  • A person presses send: Claude blocks Team users from auto-approving send tools, and ChatGPT may ask before posting to Teams.13,25 OWASP advises human confirmation for high-stakes actions.50
  • The owner of each number checks it, such as the controller for finance work. Both spreadsheet add-ins tell users to review changed cells, and NIST advises checking output.24,16,51
  • Compliance owns the record. Copilot logs each interaction to Purview Audit, Microsoft's compliance log, while Claude and ChatGPT audit logs need their enterprise tiers.34,5,23

What do teams get wrong when they choose?

Teams most often leave staff on personal accounts, switch on Copilot before fixing file permissions, and buy seats nobody uses.52,31,53

  • Leaving staff on personal accounts: 68% of surveyed workers use them, and personal ChatGPT and Claude plans may train on chats.52,20,8 Fix: buy a business plan and move people over.
  • Switching on Copilot before fixing file permissions: it sees whatever each person can open.31,33 Fix: run Microsoft's readiness check and clean up overshared sites first.38,39
  • Buying seats nobody uses: firms leave 36% of software licences unused, and AI use is 'widespread but shallow'.53,47 Fix: pilot first; Claude Team seats can be reassigned but removals aren't refunded.4
  • Budgeting seats, not usage: Copilot Cowork bills per use, and Claude Enterprise bills all usage at API rates.29,5 Fix: set firm and per-user spend limits before launch.5
  • Trusting it on analysis it's weak at: a UK government test found Copilot's Excel analysis slower and lower quality.54 Fix: name which tasks always get a human check.51
  • Letting agents act on email and files unchecked: hidden instructions in a document or email can hijack an assistant, OWASP's top AI risk.49 Fix: keep approval on for send and write actions.10,50

Questions buyers ask before they choose

Is ChatGPT Business or Claude Team better for a small team?

Claude Team for long documents and spreadsheets at 150 seats or fewer; ChatGPT Business if you may grow past 150.3,16,18 Price ties: both cost $20 a seat billed yearly and $25 monthly.17,3 Claude Team reads 200K tokens at once, against 54K on ChatGPT's fast model.3,17

Claude or Copilot for a 40-person company on Microsoft 365?

Copilot, if most work is email, meetings and SharePoint: it searches them directly and logs to Purview.31,34 Claude reaches the same data through a connector a Microsoft admin approves, and suits heavy document and Excel work.12,16

Does Microsoft Copilot use Claude?

Yes, in most US business accounts: Claude models are on by default in Copilot, Researcher and Cowork.36,37 Admins can turn them off, and they sit outside Microsoft's EU Data Boundary.36

What is the difference between ChatGPT Work and Claude Cowork?

Little in kind: both run multi-step tasks and hand back files, but Cowork brings Claude Code's agent tools to everyday office work.21,11 Both draw on the business seat's usage; Copilot's Cowork bills per use on top.60,3,29

Will ChatGPT, Claude or Copilot train on our company data?

Not by default on business plans, per all three vendors.20,7,32 Personal plans differ: ChatGPT and Claude may train on those chats unless the user opts out.20,8

Is Copilot safer than Claude for a company rollout?

No, not on training: neither trains on business data by default, so the gap is records.7,32 Copilot logs every interaction to Purview Audit; Claude and ChatGPT audit logs need their enterprise tiers.34,5,23

Should you buy a tool or build your own?

Buy: Buy one of these when the job is everyday drafting, summarizing and spreadsheet work for many staff.55 MIT found bought tools reached deployment about twice as often as internal builds.55 Over half of US businesses on Ramp already pay for AI this way.48

Build: Build on a vendor's API when your own data or a fixed process is the edge, such as document intake tied to your systems.55 Claude Enterprise already bills usage at API rates, which blurs the line.5

When should you pick neither?

Pick neither when light needs fit Gemini in Workspace or Copilot Chat, one process needs a purpose-built tool, or staff won't use it yet.40,31,55,47 Copilot Chat comes at no extra cost on Microsoft 365.31

  • You run Google Workspace and needs are light: Business Standard costs $14 a user a month on a one-year commitment.40 It includes Gemini in Gmail, Docs and Meet, and Google says it doesn't train on your data without permission.40,41
  • You're on Microsoft 365 and testing the water: start with Copilot Chat at no extra cost.31 Buy paid seats once usage shows who needs them.53
  • You need one repeat process fixed, such as invoice intake: a purpose-built tool beats a general assistant.55
  • Staff won't use it yet: fix the task list and training first.47 In one large field study, a three-hour onboarding came first, and newer staff gained most.42
