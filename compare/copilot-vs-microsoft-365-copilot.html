Microsoft Copilot vs Microsoft 365 Copilot: which fits your firm?

Bottom line
  • Pick Copilot Chat if staff mostly draft, search the web and summarize files they open. It comes with your plan.1,2
  • Pick Copilot Business if you have under 300 users who need answers from their own email, meetings and files.3,4
  • Pick a “with Copilot” bundle over the add-on when renewing Business Standard or Premium. The bundle costs less.5,6
  • Pick enterprise Copilot if you run E3 or E5, or have more than 300 users.7,8
  • Pick neither yet if SharePoint permissions are messy. Microsoft says fix oversharing first; Gartner found it delayed rollouts.9,10

What is the real difference?

Microsoft 365 Copilot is now named “Microsoft Copilot”, so the real choice is Copilot Chat or paid Copilot.11,1 Included Copilot Chat sees the web plus what a user opens or pastes.1,2 Paid Copilot also searches the mail, meetings and files each user can open, so permissions make or break it.3,9

Which one fits your team?

1. A 40-80 person firm on Business Standard, testing the water

Pick this when you have no pilot yet. Copilot Chat costs nothing extra, so start there and buy a few seats at the promo price.1,4

  1. IT adminMake sure everyone signs in to Copilot with a work account, and block personal-account Copilot on work devices15
  2. Ops leadRoll out Copilot Chat to everyone, with one page on what to use it for and what not to paste in
  3. Ops leadBuy Copilot Business for 10 people who live in email and meetings, such as account managers and project leads4
  4. Department headsAfter 60 to 90 days, time two named tasks, such as meeting follow-ups and proposal first drafts, against Chat-only staff. Then keep, widen or drop

Cost: 10 seats at the $18 first-year promo: $2,160 for year one, if bought by 31 Dec 2026.4 At the standard $21, it is $2,520 a year.4

2. A 150-250 person firm on Business Premium, ready for a real pilot

Pick this when leaders want answers from their own mail and files, and you can fix permissions first.9,6

  1. IT adminRun the SharePoint Advanced Management content assessment, which comes with the Copilot license27
  2. IT adminRun the Purview oversharing assessment, which scans the top 100 sites weekly by default28
  3. Site ownersReview access on flagged sites and remove links shared with the whole company or with anyone9,29
  4. Ops leadChoose about 30 pilot seats across ops, finance and sales leadership
  5. FinancePrice the Business Premium with Copilot bundle against base plan plus add-on6,17
  6. IT adminSet a spending cap for usage-billed features before the default policy starts24,30

Cost: 30 seats on the bundle: $3,600 a year more than Business Premium alone.6,31 The same 30 seats as a $21 add-on: $7,560 a year.4

3. A 300-500 person firm on E3

Pick this when you are past the 300-user cap for Copilot Business, or already on E3 or E5.4,8

  1. IT adminTurn on Copilot Chat for everyone, since E3 already includes it1
  2. Ops lead and department headsName the 40 to 60 roles with the heaviest email and meeting load
  3. FinanceBuy enterprise Copilot for those roles only7
  4. Ops leadIf staff without a seat need one internal agent, such as HR policy questions, buy one Copilot Studio pack12
  5. IT adminHide sensitive sites with Restricted Content Discovery while permissions get fixed32

Cost: 50 seats at $30 a month is $18,000 a year, plus $2,400 for one Studio pack.7,12 Total: $20,400 a year before usage-billed extras.7,12

Where does your team stay in charge?

  • Site owners and IT decide who can see what. Copilot only shows each user what they can already open, so access reviews come first.3,16,9
  • The person who owns the work signs off client emails, contract clauses, numbers and slides. Government trials found it useful for basic admin but weaker on complex work, including Excel analysis.33,34
  • Finance and IT own the spend. Admins set the cap for usage-billed features such as Cowork, Autopilot and agents.24,25
  • Every agent gets a named owner who checks what it answers and what data it can reach.20,22

What do teams get wrong when they choose?

  • Copilot surfaces whatever is already overshared, and Microsoft says fix that before rollout.9,29 In Gartner's 2024 survey of IT leaders, 40% delayed rollout three months or more over it.10
  • The names changed on 28 Oct 2026, so quotes and old documents may use either. Check the product code on the quote, not the label.11,35
  • Usage billing sits on top of the seat: Cowork, Autopilot and some agents bill per credit.24,25 New Copilot Business partner purchases get a default cap of up to $40 per user a month from early 2027, so set your own.30,24
  • Staff on personal Microsoft accounts fall outside enterprise data protection. Look for the green shield and Work label, and block personal Copilot with tenant restrictions v2.13,15
  • The promo and the bundles can skew a budget. The $18 price is year one only, and bundles cost less than base plus add-on, so price both routes.4,5,6
  • Evidence on value is mixed, so pilot one team on one timed task first. UK staff self-reported 26 minutes saved a day, but one UK department found no proof of a productivity gain.36,34

Questions buyers ask before they choose

Is Microsoft Copilot the same as Microsoft 365 Copilot?

Yes. On 28 Oct 2026 Microsoft renamed Microsoft 365 Copilot to Microsoft Copilot, and Copilot Chat to Microsoft Copilot Chat.11 The free consumer app is also called Microsoft Copilot, so check the product on the quote.11,13

Is Copilot Chat as secure as the paid license for internal documents?

Yes, on data protection: both get it with a work sign-in, and prompts are not used to train foundation models.16 The license changes what Copilot can reach, not the protection.21 Web searches it sends to Bing fall outside Microsoft's data protection addendum.16

What's the difference between Copilot Chat and Microsoft 365 Copilot?

Copilot Chat knows the web plus what a user uploads or has open.1,2 Paid Copilot also searches the email, meetings, chats and files that user can already open.3 One buyer on Reddit put it as “the free one knows the internet and the paid one knows your company”.37,38

Do we need Copilot Studio, or does Copilot Chat cover agents?

Copilot Chat includes agents built on instructions or public websites; agents on company data are pay-as-you-go and off by default.22 You need Copilot Studio to publish agents to websites, apps or staff without a paid seat.12,20,39

Half my team has no paid license. Can I build them agents instead?

Yes, through pay-as-you-go agents in Copilot Chat.22 A grounded answer costs 12 credits, or $0.12, in Microsoft's example.19,25 As a rough guide, past about 175 such answers a month per person, a $21 seat is cheaper.19,4

Is there a minimum seat count for Copilot?

No. Copilot Business sells from 1 to 300 seats, and enterprise Copilot has had no minimum since January 2024.4,17,18

Should you buy a tool or build your own?

Buy: Buy for this job. Copilot runs inside the Microsoft 365 apps you already pay for, under the same data protection.3,16 Most enterprise AI is now bought: 76% of use cases in Menlo Ventures' 2025 survey of 495 US decision-makers.40

Build: Build only the narrow parts, such as one or two internal agents in Copilot Studio, priced per credit.12,19 Either way, you still own the permissions clean-up, the pilot measure and the spending cap.9,24

When should you pick neither?

  • Your firm runs on Google Workspace, not Microsoft 365: use Gemini, which comes with Workspace business plans.41 Those cost $7, $14 or $22 per user a month, paid yearly (read 26 Nov 2026).41
  • Your main need is general writing and analysis, not searching your Microsoft 365 files: look at ChatGPT Business or Claude Team.42,43 Both list standard seats at $20 per user a month, paid yearly (read 26 Nov 2026).42,43
  • Only a few staff need answers from company data, a few times a day: a pay-as-you-go agent in Copilot Chat can cost less than seats.19,22
  • Your SharePoint permissions are a mess: fix oversharing before buying seats, as Microsoft's own guidance says.9,29 Microsoft's Restricted Content Discovery setting can hide sensitive sites meanwhile, but Microsoft calls it temporary.32
